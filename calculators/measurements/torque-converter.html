Torque Unit Conversion Guide

Torque is a rotational force — the tendency of a force to rotate an object around an axis. It is the product of force and the distance from the pivot point. Torque is critical in automotive engineering (engine output, lug nut tightening), structural engineering, and any mechanical system involving rotation.

Newton-Meters (Nm)

The Newton-meter (Nm) is the SI unit of torque. European cars and most international engineering specs use Nm. 1 Nm = the torque produced by 1 Newton of force applied at 1 meter from the pivot. Modern car engines produce 150-500 Nm of torque. Electric motors often produce over 1,000 Nm.

Foot-Pounds (lb-ft)

The foot-pound (lb-ft) is the US customary unit of torque. US car specs, torque wrenches sold in the US, and American engineering documents use lb-ft. 1 lb-ft = the torque from 1 pound of force at 1 foot distance = 1.3558 Nm. A typical car engine: 150-350 lb-ft of torque.

Why Torque Matters in Automotive

Torque determines how quickly a vehicle can accelerate, especially at low RPMs. More torque = better pulling power. Electric vehicles produce maximum torque from 0 RPM, which is why EVs accelerate so quickly compared to gasoline engines that build torque as RPM increases.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you convert Nm to lb-ft? +
Multiply Newton-meters by 0.7376 to get foot-pounds. Example: 200 Nm x 0.7376 = 147.5 lb-ft. To convert lb-ft to Nm, multiply by 1.3558. Example: 100 lb-ft = 135.58 Nm.
What is torque in a car engine? +
Engine torque is the twisting force the engine produces, measured at the crankshaft. Higher torque = better acceleration and towing capacity. Electric motors produce 300-1,000+ Nm from 0 RPM. Gasoline engines peak at higher RPMs.
How tight should lug nuts be? +
Most passenger cars require 80-120 lb-ft (108-163 Nm) of torque for lug nuts. Always check your vehicle owner manual for the specific torque spec. Using a calibrated torque wrench is important — overtightening can warp brake rotors; undertightening can be dangerous.
What is the difference between torque and horsepower? +
Torque is rotational force (Nm or lb-ft). Horsepower is the rate of doing work (how fast torque is applied). HP = Torque x RPM / 5,252 (in lb-ft and HP). At 5,252 RPM, a vehicle produces equal numbers of horsepower and torque (in US units).
What is kilogram-force meter (kgm)? +
1 kgm (kilogram-force meter) = 9.8067 Nm = 7.233 lb-ft. This older metric unit represents the torque of 1 kilogram of force at 1 meter distance. Still appears in some older European and Japanese specifications.
How do I use a torque wrench? +
Set the torque wrench to the specified value (e.g., 100 Nm). Tighten until the wrench clicks or beeps. Do not continue tightening after the click. Torque wrenches should be stored at their lowest setting and recalibrated annually for accuracy.
