How to Convert Weight Units

Weight conversion is needed in everyday life — from cooking recipes that use grams vs ounces, to understanding your body weight in both kilograms and pounds, to shipping packages internationally where weight limits are in different units.

The base unit of mass in the SI system is the kilogram (kg). All other mass units are defined relative to it. The kilogram was redefined in 2019 based on Planck's constant, making it the most precisely defined unit in history.

Kilograms to Pounds Conversion

The kilogram to pound conversion is the most commonly searched weight conversion worldwide. 1 kg = 2.20462 lbs. To convert kg to lbs, multiply by 2.20462. To convert lbs to kg, multiply by 0.453592.

  • 60 kg = 132.28 lbs (average adult female weight)
  • 70 kg = 154.32 lbs (average adult male weight)
  • 100 kg = 220.46 lbs

Grams to Ounces Conversion

Essential for cooking, baking, and food labeling. 1 ounce = 28.3495 grams. To convert ounces to grams, multiply by 28.3495. To convert grams to ounces, divide by 28.3495.

  • 100g = 3.527 oz
  • 500g = 17.637 oz
  • 1 pound (16 oz) = 453.592g

Stones to Kilograms

The stone is a unit of weight used in the UK and Ireland for body weight. 1 stone = 14 pounds = 6.35029 kg. A person weighing 11 stone 7 pounds = 73.03 kg.

How many pounds are in a kilogram? +

How many grams are in an ounce? +
1 ounce = 28.3495 grams exactly. 16 ounces (1 pound) = 453.592 grams. 1 kg = 35.274 ounces. This conversion is critical for cooking and food nutrition labels.
What is a stone in kg? +
1 stone = 14 pounds = 6.35029 kilograms. The stone is still commonly used for body weight in the UK and Ireland. A person of 70 kg weighs approximately 11 stone.
What is the difference between mass and weight? +
Mass is the amount of matter in an object (measured in kg). Weight is the force gravity exerts on that mass (measured in Newtons). On Earth they are proportional, but on the Moon your weight would be 1/6th your Earth weight while your mass stays the same.
How do I convert my body weight from lbs to kg? +
Multiply pounds by 0.453592 to get kilograms. For example: 150 lbs × 0.453592 = 68.04 kg. Or divide pounds by 2.205 for a quick estimate.
How many milligrams are in a gram? +
There are exactly 1,000 milligrams (mg) in 1 gram (g). Milligrams are used for medications, vitamins, and precise chemistry measurements.
