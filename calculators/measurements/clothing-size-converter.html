Clothing Size Conversion Guide

Clothing sizes vary dramatically between countries and even between different brands within the same country. A US size 8 woman is EU 38 = UK 12. A US men Medium = EU 48-50. Understanding international size conversion is essential for shopping online from overseas retailers and fashion brands.

Women Clothing Sizes

US sizes run numerically (0, 2, 4, 6, 8...) while EU uses even numbers (32, 34, 36, 38...) and UK is different again (4, 6, 8, 10, 12...). EU = US + 30 approximately. UK = US + 4 approximately. Sizes are based on bust, waist, and hip measurements in inches or centimeters.

Men Clothing Sizes

Men use letter sizes (XS, S, M, L, XL) or numeric chest sizes (36, 38, 40, 42...) in inches. EU numeric = US/UK chest inches + 10. A US/UK 40-inch chest = EU 50. Shirt collar sizes: US 15 = EU 38, US 16 = EU 41.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is US size 8 in EU? +
US women size 8 = EU 38 = UK 12. This is approximately a 29-30 inch waist and 38-39 inch hip measurement. EU sizes = US size + 30 as a general rule, though variations exist by brand.
What is Medium in EU clothing? +
For women: US M = EU 38-40 = UK 12-14. For men: US M = EU 48-50 = UK/US 38-40 inch chest. EU men sizing is based on chest measurement in centimeters. US M typically fits a 38-40 inch chest.
How do I measure for clothing sizes? +
Bust: measure around fullest part of chest. Waist: measure around narrowest part of torso. Hip: measure around fullest part of hips, about 8 inches below waist. Always measure over thin clothing for accuracy.
Why do clothing sizes differ between brands? +
Vanity sizing (making sizes smaller than actual measurements) means a brand size 8 today fits measurements that were a size 12-14 decades ago. Always check specific brand size charts and measurements rather than relying on size numbers alone.
What is the difference between UK and US clothing sizes? +
For women: UK size = US size + 4. US size 8 = UK size 12. US size 0 = UK size 4. For men, sizes are similar but collar and sleeve measurements can differ slightly.
What is Asian sizing vs US sizing? +
Asian (Japanese, Korean, Chinese) clothing often runs 1-2 sizes smaller than US. A US Medium may be an Asian XL or XXL. Always check measurements in centimeters when ordering from Asian retailers online.
