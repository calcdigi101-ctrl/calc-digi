How to Convert Temperature Scales

Temperature conversion is one of the most commonly needed conversions in daily life. The United States uses the Fahrenheit scale for weather and everyday temperature, while the rest of the world uses Celsius. Scientists worldwide use the Kelvin scale for absolute temperature measurements.

Celsius to Fahrenheit Formula

The formula to convert Celsius to Fahrenheit is: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. To convert Fahrenheit to Celsius: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9.

  • 0°C = 32°F (water freezes)
  • 20°C = 68°F (comfortable room temperature)
  • 37°C = 98.6°F (normal body temperature)
  • 100°C = 212°F (water boils at sea level)

Celsius to Kelvin

Kelvin is the SI unit of temperature used in science and engineering. Unlike Celsius and Fahrenheit, Kelvin starts at absolute zero — the coldest possible temperature where all molecular motion stops. K = °C + 273.15.

  • Absolute zero: 0 K = -273.15°C = -459.67°F
  • Water freezes: 273.15 K = 0°C
  • Room temperature: 293.15 K = 20°C
  • Water boils: 373.15 K = 100°C

Quick Mental Conversion Tricks

For a quick Celsius to Fahrenheit estimate: double the Celsius and add 30. For example, 20°C → (20×2)+30 = 70°F (actual is 68°F — close enough for everyday use).

What is the formula to convert Celsius to Fahrenheit? +
Multiply Celsius by 9/5, then add 32: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. For example, 25°C = (25 × 1.8) + 32 = 45 + 32 = 77°F. To reverse: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9.
What is 37 degrees Celsius in Fahrenheit? +
37°C is 98.6°F — the normal human body temperature. Using the formula: (37 × 9/5) + 32 = 66.6 + 32 = 98.6°F. A fever in adults is generally considered 38°C (100.4°F) or above.
What is the difference between Celsius and Kelvin? +
Celsius and Kelvin use the same size degree but different zero points. 0°C = 273.15 K. To convert Celsius to Kelvin, simply add 273.15. Kelvin starts at absolute zero (-273.15°C), where all thermal motion stops.
At what temperature are Fahrenheit and Celsius equal? +
At -40 degrees — both scales read -40 at this unique point. This is because the two scales converge at exactly -40, making it the only temperature with the same value in both Celsius and Fahrenheit.
What temperature is comfortable room temperature? +
Comfortable room temperature is typically 20-22°C (68-72°F). Most HVAC systems are set to maintain 21°C (70°F) as a default comfortable indoor temperature for most people.
What is absolute zero in Celsius and Fahrenheit? +
Absolute zero is -273.15°C or -459.67°F (0 Kelvin). At this temperature, all molecular motion theoretically stops. It has never been achieved in practice, though scientists have come within billionths of a degree.
