Cooking Measurement Conversion Guide

Cooking measurements differ significantly between countries. US recipes use volume (cups, tablespoons). European and professional cooking uses weight (grams). Baking especially requires precision — a cup of flour can range from 120-160g depending on how it is scooped, which is why professional bakers always weigh ingredients.

US Volume Measurements

1 cup = 16 tablespoons = 48 teaspoons = 8 fluid ounces = 236.588 mL. Key fractions: 1/4 cup = 4 tbsp = 59.1 mL. 1/3 cup = 5 tbsp + 1 tsp = 78.9 mL. 1/2 cup = 8 tbsp = 118.3 mL. 3/4 cup = 12 tbsp = 177.4 mL.

UK vs US vs Australian Tablespoon

US tablespoon = 14.787 mL. UK tablespoon = 15 mL. Australian tablespoon = 20 mL (4 teaspoons instead of 3 — a significant difference for baking!). Always check which country a recipe comes from.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many tablespoons are in a cup? +
1 US cup = 16 tablespoons = 48 teaspoons = 8 fluid ounces = 236.588 mL. One of the most useful cooking conversions. Half a cup = 8 tablespoons. Quarter cup = 4 tablespoons.
How do I convert cups to grams for flour? +
1 cup of all-purpose flour = approximately 125 grams (spooned and leveled) or 120-130g. Directly scooping the cup into flour compacts it to 140-160g. For accurate baking, always weigh flour in grams.
What is 1 cup in mL? +
1 US cup = 236.588 mL (approximately 237 mL). The Australian/Canadian metric cup = 250 mL exactly. UK modern recipes use 250 mL cups. Always check which cup size a recipe uses.
How many teaspoons in a tablespoon? +
3 US teaspoons = 1 US tablespoon (14.787 mL). But 4 Australian teaspoons = 1 Australian tablespoon (20 mL). This difference matters in baking — always clarify the recipe origin.
How do I convert a recipe from cups to grams? +
Multiply cups by grams-per-cup for that ingredient: flour 1 cup = 125g, sugar 1 cup = 200g, butter 1 cup = 227g, rice 1 cup = 185g, milk 1 cup = 244g, honey 1 cup = 340g, cocoa 1 cup = 85g.
What is a pinch in teaspoons? +
A pinch = approximately 1/16 teaspoon (0.31 mL). A dash = 1/8 teaspoon (0.62 mL). A smidgen = 1/32 teaspoon. These are informal measurements that vary by cook and should be used only for seasonings, not for baking.
