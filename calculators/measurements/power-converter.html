Power Unit Conversion Guide

Power is the rate at which energy is transferred or converted. The SI unit is the Watt (W). Different industries use different units: electrical systems use watts and kilowatts, automotive engineering uses horsepower, HVAC uses BTU/hour, and older mechanical systems use foot-pounds per minute.

Watts and Kilowatts

1 kilowatt (kW) = 1,000 watts (W). Household appliances range from 5W (LED bulb) to 5,000W (electric oven). A typical home uses 1-5 kW average power. Solar panels are rated in kilowatts peak (kWp).

Horsepower

1 mechanical horsepower (hp) = 745.699 watts. The unit was coined by James Watt in the 18th century to compare steam engine output to horse-pulling power. A typical car engine: 100-400 hp = 75-300 kW. An electric motor is more efficient than a combustion engine in converting watts to mechanical power.

BTU per Hour for HVAC

HVAC systems use BTU/hr for heating and cooling capacity. 1 BTU/hr = 0.293071 watts. A 10,000 BTU/hr window AC = 2,930 watts. Home furnaces range from 40,000 to 120,000 BTU/hr (11.7 to 35.2 kW).

Frequently Asked Questions

How many watts are in one horsepower? +
1 mechanical horsepower = 745.699 watts. 1 metric horsepower = 735.499 watts. Electric motors are often rated in both kW and hp. A 100 hp car engine = 74.6 kW. A 200 hp engine = 149.1 kW.
How do you convert kilowatts to horsepower? +
Multiply kilowatts by 1.34102 to get mechanical horsepower. For example: 100 kW × 1.34102 = 134.1 hp. To convert hp to kW, multiply by 0.7457. A 250 hp engine = 186.4 kW.
What is the difference between kW and kWh? +
kW (kilowatt) is a unit of power — the rate of energy use at a given moment. kWh (kilowatt-hour) is a unit of energy — the total energy used over time. A 2 kW heater running for 3 hours uses 6 kWh of energy.
How many BTU/hr is 1 ton of air conditioning? +
1 ton of AC = 12,000 BTU/hr = 3,517 watts = 3.517 kW. Larger homes typically need 2-5 tons of cooling capacity. Rule of thumb: 1 ton per 500-600 sq ft in a well-insulated home.
What is a watt exactly? +
1 watt = 1 joule per second. Named after James Watt, it measures the rate of energy transfer. A 60W bulb converts 60 joules of electrical energy into light and heat every second.
How many watts does a typical house use? +
Average US home uses about 1.2 kW average power (10,500 kWh/year). Peak usage can reach 5-10 kW with AC, oven, and other appliances running simultaneously. A solar array of 5-8 kW typically covers most household needs.
