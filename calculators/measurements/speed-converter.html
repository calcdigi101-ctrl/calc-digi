Speed Converter – MPH, KPH, Knots & More

Speed Unit Conversion Guide

Speed is measured in different units depending on the context. Road speed uses mph (US, UK) or km/h (most countries). Aviation and marine navigation use knots. Physics uses meters per second. Supersonic flight uses Mach numbers.

MPH to KPH Conversion

1 mile per hour = 1.60934 km/h. To convert mph to km/h, multiply by 1.60934. Common driving speed conversions: 30 mph = 48.3 km/h, 60 mph = 96.6 km/h, 70 mph = 112.7 km/h, 100 mph = 160.9 km/h.

Knots in Aviation and Navigation

A knot (kn) = 1 nautical mile per hour = 1.852 km/h = 1.15078 mph. Knots are used universally in aviation (airspeed) and marine navigation (boat speed). Commercial aircraft cruise at about 500 knots (926 km/h, 575 mph).

Mach Number — Speed of Sound

Mach 1 is the speed of sound: approximately 343 m/s = 1,235 km/h = 767 mph at sea level at 20°C. At altitude where commercial aircraft fly, the speed of sound is lower (~295 m/s at 35,000 ft). Supersonic aircraft fly above Mach 1.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you convert mph to km/h? +
Multiply mph by 1.60934 to get km/h. Quick estimate: multiply by 1.6. For example: 60 mph × 1.60934 = 96.56 km/h ≈ 97 km/h. To convert km/h to mph, divide by 1.60934.
What is a knot in speed? +
A knot equals 1 nautical mile per hour = 1.852 km/h = 1.15078 mph. Knots are the standard speed unit in aviation and marine navigation. A ship traveling at 20 knots moves at 37 km/h (23 mph).
What is Mach 1 in mph? +
Mach 1 (the speed of sound) at sea level and 20°C is approximately 767 mph (1,235 km/h, 343 m/s). At high altitude where air is thinner and colder, the speed of sound decreases to about 660 mph.
How do you convert m/s to mph? +
Multiply meters per second by 2.23694 to get mph. For example: 10 m/s × 2.23694 = 22.37 mph. Or multiply m/s by 3.6 to get km/h, then divide by 1.60934 to get mph.
What is the fastest speed ever recorded? +
The fastest human-made object is the Parker Solar Probe, which reached speeds of over 690,000 km/h (429,000 mph) as it orbited close to the Sun. The fastest manned aircraft was the X-15 at Mach 6.7 (7,274 km/h).
What speed is considered supersonic? +
Supersonic speed is any speed above Mach 1 (the speed of sound). Hypersonic is above Mach 5 (about 6,175 km/h). Commercial aircraft fly at about Mach 0.85 — just below the speed of sound.
