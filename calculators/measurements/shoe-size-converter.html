Conversion Formula

Shoe Size Conversion Guide

Shoe sizing varies significantly between countries. A US men size 10 = UK 9.5 = EU 43-44. Shopping internationally or online without knowing your size in different systems often leads to poorly fitting shoes. This guide helps you convert accurately.

US Men vs Women Sizing

In the US, women sizes are 1.5 sizes larger than men for the same foot length. Men US 8 = Women US 9.5 for the same foot. Both use barleycorn (1/3 inch) increments starting from different baselines.

European (EU) Sizing

EU uses Paris Points (2/3 cm each). EU 42 = approximately 26.7 cm foot length. EU sizing is gender-neutral — the same scale applies to both men and women, unlike the US system which has separate scales.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is US shoe size 10 in EU? +
US Men 10 = EU 43-44, approximately 27.3-27.6 cm foot length. US Women 10 = EU 41. Sizes can vary by brand and shoe model, so checking specific brand size charts is always recommended.
What is EU shoe size 42 in US? +
EU 42 = US Men 8.5-9 = US Women 10-10.5 = UK 8. EU 42 corresponds to approximately 26.7 cm (10.5 inches) foot length. EU sizes are the same for men and women.
How do I measure my shoe size at home? +
Stand on paper and trace your foot. Measure heel to longest toe in centimeters. US Men size = (foot length in inches - 0.66) / 0.33 + 1. US Women = Men size + 1.5. Round to nearest half size.
Why do men and women have different US shoe sizes? +
US shoe sizing evolved separately for men and women with different baseline measurements. The result is a 1.5 US size gap for the same foot length. Most other countries use a single unisex scale (EU, UK, Japanese).
What do shoe width letters mean? +
Men widths: B (narrow), D (standard), 2E/EE (wide), 4E/EEEE (extra wide). Women widths: AA (narrow), B (standard), D (wide). Most shoes are made in standard width. Wide widths are important for comfort and foot health.
Are different shoe brands true to size? +
Most major brands (Nike, Adidas, New Balance) are close to standard sizing but can differ by half a size between models. Running shoes are typically worn 0.5-1 size larger than dress shoes to accommodate toe spread during activity.
