How to Convert Length Units

Length conversion is one of the most common unit conversions in daily life. Whether you are converting your height from centimeters to feet and inches, calculating the distance of a road trip from miles to kilometers, or working on a construction project that requires switching between metric and imperial measurements — a reliable length converter is essential.

All length conversions are based on a single reference unit: the meter, which is the SI (International System of Units) base unit for length. Every other unit is defined in terms of meters.

1 inch = 2.54 cm (exact) | 1 foot = 0.3048 m | 1 mile = 1.60934 km | 1 yard = 0.9144 m

To convert from any unit to another, first convert to meters, then convert from meters to the target unit. Our length converter does this automatically and instantly.

Most Common Length Conversions

Meters to Feet Conversion

The meters to feet conversion is one of the most searched unit conversions globally, used by engineers, architects, athletes, and travelers. To convert meters to feet, multiply by 3.28084. To convert feet to meters, multiply by 0.3048.

  • 1 meter = 3.28084 feet
  • 5 meters = 16.4042 feet
  • 10 meters = 32.8084 feet
  • 100 meters = 328.084 feet

Inches to Centimeters Conversion

The inch-to-centimeter conversion is used daily for screen sizes, clothing measurements, and product dimensions. The conversion factor is exact: 1 inch = 2.54 cm.

  • 6 inches = 15.24 cm
  • 12 inches (1 foot) = 30.48 cm
  • 24 inches (2 feet) = 60.96 cm

Miles to Kilometers Conversion

The miles to kilometers conversion is essential for international travel, marathon distances, and speed conversions. 1 mile = 1.60934 km.

  • 5 miles = 8.047 km (a common road race distance)
  • 26.2 miles = 42.195 km (marathon)
  • 60 mph = 96.56 km/h

Height Conversion: Feet/Inches to Centimeters

Height is expressed in feet and inches in the US and UK, but in centimeters in most of the world. To convert 5 feet 10 inches to cm: (5 × 30.48) + (10 × 2.54) = 152.4 + 25.4 = 177.8 cm.

Metric vs Imperial Length Units

The world uses two main measurement systems for length: the metric system (meters, kilometers, centimeters) used by most countries, and the imperial/US customary system (feet, inches, miles, yards) primarily used in the United States, Myanmar, and Liberia.

The metric system is base-10, making calculations straightforward — 1 km = 1,000 m = 100,000 cm. The imperial system uses irregular relationships (12 inches per foot, 3 feet per yard, 1,760 yards per mile), which is why conversions require specific factors.

Scientists and engineers worldwide use SI (metric) units exclusively. However, the imperial system remains deeply embedded in everyday US life — road signs, body height, clothing sizes, and real estate measurements all use feet and inches in America.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many feet are in a meter? +
1 meter = 3.28084 feet. To quickly estimate, remember that 1 meter is approximately 3 feet 3 inches. Multiply meters by 3.28084 to get feet, or multiply feet by 0.3048 to get meters.
How many centimeters are in an inch? +
Exactly 2.54 centimeters = 1 inch. This is the official international definition. So 12 inches = 30.48 cm, 36 inches (1 yard) = 91.44 cm, and 63,360 inches (1 mile) = 160,934.4 cm.
How do you convert miles to kilometers? +
Multiply miles by 1.60934 to get kilometers. A quick mental estimate: multiply miles by 1.6. For example: 10 miles ≈ 16 km, 100 miles ≈ 161 km, 26.2 miles (marathon) = 42.195 km exactly.
What is 5 foot 7 inches in centimeters? +
5 feet 7 inches = (5 × 30.48) + (7 × 2.54) = 152.4 + 17.78 = 170.18 cm. This is close to the world average height for men.
What is the difference between a mile and a nautical mile? +
A statute mile = 1,609.344 meters. A nautical mile = 1,852 meters (about 15% longer). Nautical miles are used in aviation and marine navigation because 1 nautical mile equals 1 minute of arc of latitude on Earth's surface.
How do I convert my height from cm to feet? +
Divide your height in cm by 30.48 to get feet. The decimal part converts to inches by multiplying by 12. For 175 cm: 175 ÷ 30.48 = 5.741 feet = 5 feet + (0.741 × 12) = 5 feet 8.9 inches ≈ 5 ft 9 in.
