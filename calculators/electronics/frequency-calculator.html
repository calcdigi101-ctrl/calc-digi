Frequency Calculator – Hz, Period and Wavelength

Calculate frequency, period, and wavelength for any wave — audio, radio, light, or electromagnetic. Convert between hertz, kilohertz, megahertz, and gigahertz. Essential for electronics engineers, radio operators, and physics students.

From the 60 Hz hum of AC power to the 5 GHz channels of WiFi 6, from the 20 Hz bass of a subwoofer to the 3 GHz operating speed of a modern CPU — frequency is the universal language of electronics and physics. Every oscillating signal, every periodic phenomenon, and every communication channel is defined by its frequency.

Formula

f = 1 / T | T = 1 / f | λ = c / f (c = 3×10⁸ m/s for EM waves)

Frequency, Period and Wavelength Guide

Frequency (f) is the number of cycles per second, measured in Hertz (Hz). Period (T) is the time for one complete cycle in seconds. Wavelength (λ) is the physical distance between wave crests. These three quantities describe every wave in nature — from audio to radio to light.

Frequency and Period Relationship

Frequency and period are exact reciprocals: f = 1/T and T = 1/f. A 50 Hz power supply completes 50 cycles per second, so each cycle takes T = 1/50 = 0.02 seconds (20 milliseconds). A 1 MHz radio signal completes 1,000,000 cycles per second, with T = 1 microsecond per cycle.

Wavelength Calculation

For electromagnetic waves (radio, light): λ = c/f where c = 299,792,458 m/s (speed of light). For sound waves: λ = v/f where v ≈ 343 m/s at 20°C. A 100 MHz FM radio signal has wavelength = 3×10⁸/10⁸ = 3 meters. A 440 Hz (A4 musical note) sound wave has wavelength = 343/440 = 0.78 meters.

Frequency in Electronics and Communications

Frequency is the fundamental parameter that defines radio bands, audio ranges, processor speeds, and communication protocols. Different frequency ranges have distinct propagation characteristics, applications, and regulatory requirements.

Radio Frequency Spectrum

The RF spectrum is divided into bands: LF (30-300 kHz, AM navigation), MF (300kHz-3MHz, AM radio), HF (3-30 MHz, shortwave, amateur radio), VHF (30-300 MHz, FM radio, aircraft), UHF (300MHz-3GHz, TV, WiFi, 4G), SHF (3-30 GHz, 5G, radar, satellite). Each band has unique propagation: HF reflects off the ionosphere enabling global communication; UHF is line-of-sight only.

Crystal Oscillators and Timing

Quartz crystal oscillators provide extremely precise frequencies for clocks and communication systems. A 32.768 kHz crystal is used in watches (2^15 = 32768, convenient for binary counters). A 16 MHz crystal drives many Arduino microcontrollers. Crystals maintain frequency accuracy to ±50 ppm over temperature — far better than RC oscillators (±5%).

Nyquist Theorem and Sampling

The Nyquist theorem states that to accurately digitize an analog signal, the sampling rate must be at least 2× the highest frequency. Audio CD quality uses 44.1 kHz sampling (covers up to 22.05 kHz — beyond human hearing at 20 kHz). 5G uses 100 MHz bandwidth channels requiring multi-GHz sampling rates in the baseband processors.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the relationship between frequency and period? +
Frequency and period are reciprocals: f = 1/T and T = 1/f. If a wave has a frequency of 100 Hz, its period is 1/100 = 0.01 seconds (10 ms). If the period is 2 milliseconds, frequency = 1/0.002 = 500 Hz.
How do you calculate wavelength from frequency? +
For electromagnetic waves: wavelength (m) = speed of light (3×10⁸ m/s) / frequency (Hz). For a 100 MHz FM signal: λ = 300,000,000 / 100,000,000 = 3 meters. For sound at 20°C: λ = 343 / frequency.
What is 1 GHz in Hz? +
1 GHz (gigahertz) = 1,000,000,000 Hz = 1,000 MHz = 1,000,000 kHz. WiFi 2.4 GHz = 2,400,000,000 Hz. CPU clock speeds are measured in GHz — a 3.5 GHz processor performs 3.5 billion clock cycles per second.
What frequency is the human hearing range? +
Human hearing spans approximately 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz (20 kHz). Infrasound is below 20 Hz (felt, not heard). Ultrasound is above 20 kHz — used in medical imaging (1-15 MHz) and distance sensors.
What is the frequency of visible light? +
Visible light ranges from about 400 THz (red, 750nm wavelength) to 790 THz (violet, 380nm). 1 THz = 10¹² Hz. Green light at 550nm has frequency = 3×10⁸ / 550×10⁻⁹ = 545 THz.
Why do radio stations use MHz for frequency? +
Radio frequencies are in the MHz range: AM radio 530-1700 kHz, FM radio 87.5-108 MHz, TV broadcast 54-890 MHz. These frequencies have wavelengths suitable for long-distance propagation using practical antenna sizes.
