Home Equity Calculator

Calculate your current home equity — the difference between your home value and remaining mortgage balance. Find your loan-to-value (LTV) ratio and estimate how much you could borrow through a HELOC or home equity loan.

Formula

Home Equity = Current Home Value - Remaining Mortgage Balance LTV = Mortgage Balance / Home Value x 100

Home equity builds over time through mortgage payments and home value appreciation. Lenders typically allow you to borrow up to 85% of your home value minus the outstanding mortgage balance — known as the combined loan-to-value (CLTV) limit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is home equity? +
Home equity is the portion of your home you actually own — the difference between the current market value and your remaining mortgage balance. If your home is worth $400,000 and you owe $250,000, your equity is $150,000.
How much can I borrow against my home equity? +
Most lenders allow a combined loan-to-value (CLTV) of 85%. If your home is worth $400,000 and you owe $250,000: max borrowing = ($400,000 x 0.85) - $250,000 = $340,000 - $250,000 = $90,000.
What is a HELOC vs home equity loan? +
A HELOC (Home Equity Line of Credit) works like a credit card — draw as needed during a draw period. A home equity loan is a lump sum at a fixed rate. HELOCs have variable rates; home equity loans have fixed rates.
What is a good LTV ratio for a mortgage? +
LTV below 80% means no PMI and best rates. LTV of 80-90% is generally accepted with PMI or slightly higher rates. LTV above 90% typically requires FHA financing or other alternatives with higher rates and fees.
