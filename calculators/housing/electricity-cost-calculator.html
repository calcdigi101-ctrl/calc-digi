Electricity Cost Calculator

Calculate the electricity cost of any appliance or device based on wattage, hours of use per day, and your electricity rate. Identify which home devices are costing you the most on your monthly bill.

Formula

Daily Cost = Wattage / 1000 x Hours per Day x Cost per kWh Monthly Cost = Daily Cost x 30.5

Average US electricity rate is about $0.16 per kWh. A 1,000W appliance running for 1 hour uses 1 kWh. Your monthly bill is the sum of all appliance costs. Heating and cooling typically account for 50-70% of home electricity use.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate electricity cost for an appliance? +
Multiply the wattage by hours of use per day, divide by 1,000 to get kWh, then multiply by your electricity rate. For example: 100W bulb x 6 hours x $0.16/kWh = 0.096 kWh x $0.16 = $0.0154/day = $0.47/month.
What uses the most electricity in a home? +
Heating and cooling: 40-50%, water heater: 14-18%, washer/dryer: 13-15%, lighting: 9-12%, refrigerator: 4-8%, entertainment (TV, gaming): 3-5%. An older HVAC system is usually the biggest energy cost driver.
How much electricity does a typical house use per month? +
The average US home uses about 886 kWh per month, with higher usage in summer and winter due to climate control. Monthly electricity bills average $130-200 depending on location and home size.
How can I reduce my electricity bill? +
Switch to LED bulbs (save $8-12/year per bulb), upgrade to Energy Star appliances, use a programmable thermostat (save 10-15%), seal air leaks, add insulation, and wash clothes in cold water.
