Square Footage Calculator

Calculate total square footage of any room or entire house. Add up to 5 rooms and get total area in square feet and square meters. Essential for flooring, painting, and real estate decisions.

Formula

Square Feet = Length (ft) x Width (ft) | Total = Room1 + Room2 + ... + Room5

Square footage is the total floor area measured in square feet. Measure each room at its widest points and multiply length by width. Add room areas together for the total house square footage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate square footage of a room? +
Measure length and width in feet, then multiply: Area = Length x Width. For an L-shaped room, divide into two rectangles, calculate each area, and add them.
How many square feet is a typical house? +
Average new US homes are about 2,300 sq ft. A studio apartment: 400-600 sq ft. A 3-bedroom house: 1,200-2,000 sq ft. A luxury home: 3,000+ sq ft.
How do I convert square feet to square meters? +
Multiply square feet by 0.0929 to get square meters. Or divide by 10.764. Example: 1,000 sq ft = 92.9 square meters.
Why is square footage important in real estate? +
Square footage determines price per square foot, compares homes accurately, affects property tax assessment, determines heating/cooling needs, and helps plan furniture and renovation materials.
