Rent vs Buy Calculator

Compare the true total cost of renting vs buying a home over your expected time horizon. Accounts for home appreciation, equity buildup, opportunity cost, taxes, and maintenance.

Formula

Buy Cost = Mortgage + Tax + Insurance + Maintenance - Equity - Appreciation Rent Cost = Monthly Rent x Months + Insurance

Buying is not always better than renting. It depends on how long you stay, local appreciation rates, and your opportunity cost of the down payment. This calculator shows the true total cost of each option.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it always better to buy than rent? +
No. Buying makes more financial sense the longer you stay. In high-cost markets, renting may be cheaper for 5-10 years when you factor in opportunity cost of the down payment and 5-6% agent commissions when selling.
What is the break-even point? +
The break-even point is when total buying costs equal total renting costs. In most markets this ranges from 3-7 years. Before that point, renting is often cheaper financially.
What is opportunity cost of a down payment? +
Instead of a down payment, that money could grow in the stock market. This potential investment return is the opportunity cost of buying and must be considered in a true rent vs buy comparison.
What costs does buying include beyond the mortgage? +
Property taxes (0.5-2.5%/yr), homeowners insurance (~$1,200/yr), maintenance (1%/yr), HOA fees, closing costs (2-5%), and agent commissions when selling (5-6%).
