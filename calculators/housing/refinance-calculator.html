Frequently Asked Questions

When should I refinance? +
Refinancing makes sense when you can lower rate by 0.5-1%+, plan to stay past the break-even point, want to switch from ARM to fixed, need cash out for home improvements, or want to change loan term.
What are typical refinance closing costs? +
Refinance closing costs are typically 2-5% of loan amount, covering appraisal, title insurance, origination fees, and recording fees. No-closing-cost refinances roll fees into a slightly higher rate.
What is the break-even point? +
Break-even = closing costs divided by monthly savings. If closing costs are $6,000 and you save $200/month, break-even is 30 months. If you plan to stay longer, refinancing makes sense.
Does refinancing restart the loan term? +
Yes, a new 30-year refinance restarts the clock. If you have 20 years left, consider a 20-year refinance to avoid paying more total interest even at a lower rate.
