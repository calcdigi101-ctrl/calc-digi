Flooring Calculator

Calculate exactly how much flooring material you need including waste allowance. Enter room dimensions and waste percentage to get the total flooring required and estimated material cost.

Formula

Total Flooring = (Length x Width) x (1 + Waste%) | Total Cost = Total Flooring x Price per sq ft

Always order 10% extra for straight-lay rectangular rooms and 15% for diagonal patterns or L-shaped rooms. Measure rooms at their widest points. Include closets and doorway areas in your measurement.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much extra flooring should I order? +
Order 10% extra for rectangular rooms with straight-lay installation, 15% for diagonal cuts or complex shapes, and 20% for herringbone or chevron patterns. Extra material covers cutting waste and future repairs.
How do I measure a room for flooring? +
Measure the longest length and widest width in feet. For L-shaped rooms, split into two rectangles and add both areas. Always measure at the widest points even if there are alcoves or cutouts.
How much does flooring cost per square foot? +
Carpet: $2-8/sqft, vinyl plank: $2-7/sqft, laminate: $3-8/sqft, engineered hardwood: $5-14/sqft, solid hardwood: $8-20/sqft, ceramic tile: $5-15/sqft (not including installation labor).
Can I install flooring myself? +
Vinyl plank, laminate, and peel-and-stick tile are good DIY options. Solid hardwood and ceramic tile usually need professional installation. DIY saves $2-5/sqft in labor costs.
