Concrete Calculator

Calculate exactly how much concrete you need for slabs, driveways, patios, footings, and columns. Get results in cubic yards and number of concrete bags (60 lb or 80 lb) needed for your project.

Formula

Volume (cu yd) = Length (ft) x Width (ft) x Thickness (ft) / 27

One cubic yard of concrete = 27 cubic feet. Add 10% extra for waste and uneven subgrade. A 60-lb bag yields about 0.45 cubic feet; an 80-lb bag yields about 0.60 cubic feet of concrete.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many bags of concrete do I need? +
A 60-lb bag yields 0.45 cubic feet and an 80-lb bag yields 0.60 cubic feet. Divide total cubic feet by the yield per bag. For example, 27 cubic feet (1 yard) requires 60 bags of 60-lb concrete.
How thick should a concrete slab be? +
Standard driveway and sidewalk slabs should be at least 4 inches thick. Slabs supporting heavy vehicles need 6 inches. Garage floors are typically 4-6 inches. Patios are usually 3.5-4 inches thick.
What is the difference between concrete and cement? +
Cement is an ingredient in concrete. Concrete is a mixture of cement, sand, gravel, and water. You mix concrete; cement is just one component. Ready-mix concrete from a truck is pre-mixed; bagged products are mixed on site.
How long does concrete take to cure? +
Concrete reaches 70% strength in 7 days and full design strength in 28 days. It is safe to walk on after 24-48 hours and drive on after 7 days. Colder temperatures slow curing; never pour in freezing conditions.
