Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate the area of an L-shaped room? +
Divide the L-shape into two rectangles. Measure and calculate each rectangle separately, then add the two areas together. For example: 12x10 + 8x6 = 120 + 48 = 168 square feet.
What size area rug should I buy for my room? +
For a living room, the rug should be large enough for at least the front legs of all furniture to sit on it. Common sizes: 8x10 ft for medium rooms, 9x12 ft for large rooms. Leave 18 inches of bare floor around the perimeter.
How do I calculate room area for air conditioning? +
A general rule is 20 BTUs per square foot. A 300 sq ft room needs a 6,000 BTU AC unit. Add 10% for sunny rooms, subtract 10% for shaded rooms, and add 600 BTUs for each additional person regularly in the space.
How many square feet is a standard bedroom? +
A small bedroom is 100-130 sq ft, a standard bedroom is 130-180 sq ft, a large bedroom is 180-250 sq ft, and a primary bedroom suite is 250-400+ sq ft. Minimum livable bedroom size is typically 70-80 sq ft.
