Formula

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum down payment? +
Conventional loans: as low as 3% for first-time buyers. FHA loans: 3.5% with 580+ credit score. VA loans (veterans) and USDA loans (rural): 0% down. Jumbo loans typically require 10-20%.
What is PMI and how do I avoid it? +
PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance) is required with less than 20% down on conventional loans. It costs 0.5-1.5%/year of loan amount. Avoid by putting 20%+ down or using a piggyback 80/10/10 loan structure.
How much should I save for down payment? +
20% down avoids PMI and gets best rates. However, putting all savings into a down payment leaves you cash-poor. Many advisors suggest 10-15% down with 3-6 months emergency reserves.
Are there down payment assistance programs? +
Yes. Many states, cities, and nonprofits offer down payment grants, low-interest loans, and forgivable loans for first-time buyers. HUD-approved housing counselors can identify programs in your area.
