Paint Calculator

Calculate exactly how many gallons of paint you need for walls and ceiling. Accounts for doors and windows, number of coats, and standard paint coverage rates to give you an accurate shopping list.

Formula

Paint Needed = Wall Area / Coverage per Gallon x Number of Coats

One gallon of paint typically covers 350-400 sq ft per coat on smooth walls. Calculate wall area as: perimeter x wall height, then subtract 20 sq ft per door and 15 sq ft per window. Always buy 10% extra for touch-ups.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much wall area does 1 gallon cover? +
One gallon covers 350-400 sq ft on smooth walls with one coat. Textured or porous surfaces absorb more paint, reducing coverage to 250-300 sq ft. Premium paints may cover up to 400 sq ft per gallon.
How many coats of paint do I need? +
Painting over a similar color requires 1-2 coats. Dark-to-light or light-to-dark changes need 2-3 coats. New drywall needs primer plus 2 finish coats. Using quality paint reduces the coats needed.
What sheen should I use for walls? +
Eggshell (slight sheen) is best for living rooms and bedrooms. Satin works well for kitchens and hallways. Semi-gloss is ideal for bathrooms, trim, and doors. Flat paint hides imperfections but is harder to clean.
Should I use primer? +
Yes, always use primer on new drywall, stained surfaces, or dramatic color changes. Primer improves adhesion, coverage, and the final color accuracy. It typically saves money by reducing the number of finish coats needed.
