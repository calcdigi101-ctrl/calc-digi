Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a kitchen renovation cost? +
Kitchen renovations average $100-175/sq ft mid-range and $300-500+ for high-end. A 200 sq ft kitchen typically costs $20,000-35,000 for mid-range work including cabinets, countertops, and appliances.
How much does a bathroom renovation cost? +
Bathroom renovations average $150-250/sq ft. A 50 sq ft bathroom costs $7,500-12,500 for mid-range. Full primary bathroom renovations with new tile, fixtures, vanity, and shower often run $15,000-30,000.
How much contingency should I budget? +
Always budget 15-20% above your contractor quote for contingency. Hidden issues like mold, old wiring, or structural problems are common in older homes and can significantly increase final costs.
Does renovation increase home value? +
Kitchen and bathroom renovations typically return 60-80% of cost in added value. Curb appeal projects (exterior paint, landscaping) often return 90-100%. ROI varies significantly by local market conditions.
