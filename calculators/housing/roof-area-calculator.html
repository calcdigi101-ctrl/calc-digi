Roof Area Calculator

Calculate the total area of your roof including pitch factor. Find how many roofing squares and bundles of shingles you need for gable, hip, or flat roofs. Essential for roofing cost estimates.

Formula

Roof Area = Base Area x Pitch Factor Pitch Factor = sqrt(1 + (rise/run)^2)

Roofing is measured in squares — 1 roofing square = 100 sq ft. A bundle of shingles covers approximately 33 sq ft, so 3 bundles = 1 square. Always add 10-15% for waste at valleys, hips, and edges.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a roofing square? +
A roofing square equals 100 square feet of roof area. It is the standard unit for measuring and pricing roofing materials and labor. A typical 2,000 sq ft house roof might be 20-30 squares depending on the pitch.
How many bundles of shingles do I need per square? +
Standard 3-tab and architectural shingles require 3 bundles per roofing square (100 sq ft). Some premium shingles require 4 bundles per square. Each bundle covers approximately 33 square feet.
What is roof pitch and how does it affect area? +
Roof pitch is the rise per 12 inches of run. A 4/12 pitch rises 4 inches for every 12 inches of horizontal distance. Steeper pitches require more material — a 12/12 pitch has 41% more area than a flat roof of the same footprint.
How long does a roof last? +
3-tab shingles: 15-25 years. Architectural shingles: 25-30 years. Metal roofing: 40-70 years. Clay/concrete tile: 50+ years. Slate: 75-100+ years. Regular inspections and maintenance significantly extend roof life.
