Mortgage Calculator

Calculate your exact monthly mortgage payment, total interest paid over the loan term, and the principal vs interest split. Essential for home buying decisions and refinancing comparisons.

Formula

M = P x [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1]

Where M=monthly payment, P=principal loan amount, r=monthly interest rate (annual/12), n=total payments (years x 12). Example: $300,000 at 7% for 30 years = $1,996/month.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a mortgage payment calculated? +
Monthly payment = P x [r(1+r)^n]/[(1+r)^n-1], where P is principal, r is monthly interest rate, and n is total months. For a $300,000 loan at 7% for 30 years, the monthly payment is $1,996.
What is the difference between principal and interest? +
Principal is the original loan amount. Interest is the cost of borrowing, charged as a percentage of remaining balance. Early payments are mostly interest; later payments shift toward principal reduction.
What is a down payment? +
A down payment is the upfront cash paid when buying a home, subtracted from the purchase price. Most lenders require 3-20% down. Putting down 20% avoids PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance), saving $100-200/month.
What is amortization? +
Amortization is paying off a loan through scheduled payments where each payment covers interest on the remaining balance plus some principal. Over time, the interest portion decreases and the principal portion increases.
