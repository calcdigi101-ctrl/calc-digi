Understanding PMI Costs

If your down payment is below 20%, you'll pay Private Mortgage Insurance, typically 0.5-1.5% of the loan amount annually. On a $300,000 loan, that's $1,500-$4,500 per year ($125-$375/month) until you reach 20% equity.

Do I need 20% down to buy a house? +
No. Many loans allow 3-5% down. However, less than 20% down typically requires PMI, which adds to your monthly costs until you build enough equity. 20% down avoids PMI and often gets better interest rates.
What other costs come with a down payment? +
Closing costs typically add 2-5% of the loan amount on top of your down payment. This covers appraisal fees, title insurance, loan origination fees, and prepaid items like property taxes and homeowners insurance.
Can I use a gift for my down payment? +
Yes, most loan programs allow gift funds from family members for down payments, with proper documentation (a gift letter). Some loan types have restrictions on gift fund percentages, so check with your lender.
How fast can PMI be removed? +
Once you reach 20% equity (through payments or appreciation), you can request PMI removal. Lenders are required to automatically cancel PMI at 22% equity based on original value, per the Homeowners Protection Act.
