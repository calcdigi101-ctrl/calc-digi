Take Home Pay Calculator

Estimate your net take-home pay after federal tax, state tax, and FICA deductions (Social Security + Medicare). Get a realistic picture of what actually lands in your bank account.

Standard Payroll Deductions

  • Federal Income Tax: Based on 2025 IRS tax brackets and filing status
  • Social Security Tax: 6.2% up to the annual wage base limit ($168,600 for 2024, adjusted annually)
  • Medicare Tax: 1.45% on all wages, plus 0.9% additional on income over $200,000 (single)
  • State Income Tax: Varies widely — 0% in states like Texas and Florida, up to 13.3% in California
Take Home Pay = Gross Pay − Federal Tax − State Tax − FICA

Why is my take-home pay so much lower than my salary? +
Federal tax, FICA (7.65%), and possibly state tax combine to typically reduce gross pay by 20-35% depending on income level and location. Pre-tax deductions like 401k and health insurance further reduce take-home pay but provide benefits.
What is the FICA tax rate? +
FICA totals 7.65%: 6.2% Social Security (up to wage cap) + 1.45% Medicare (no cap). Self-employed individuals pay both employee and employer portions (15.3% total) via self-employment tax.
Does this account for 401k contributions? +
This calculator shows basic tax deductions. Pre-tax 401k contributions reduce your taxable income, which lowers federal/state tax but the contribution itself still reduces your take-home cash, just with a tax benefit.
