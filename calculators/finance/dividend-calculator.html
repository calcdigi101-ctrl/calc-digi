Understanding Dividend Yield

Dividend Yield = Annual Dividend per Share ÷ Share Price × 100
Annual Income = Shares Owned × Dividend per Share

Dividend reinvestment (DRIP) compounds returns by using dividend payments to buy additional shares automatically, which then generate their own dividends — creating a snowball effect over time.

What is a good dividend yield? +
2-5% is generally considered healthy. Extremely high yields (8%+) can signal financial distress or an unsustainable payout, so research the company's payout ratio and earnings stability.
Should I reinvest dividends or take cash? +
Reinvesting (DRIP) compounds growth and is ideal during the accumulation phase before retirement. Taking cash makes sense if you need current income, such as in retirement.
How are dividends taxed? +
Qualified dividends get favorable long-term capital gains tax rates (0-20%). Non-qualified dividends are taxed as ordinary income. Holding dividend stocks in tax-advantaged accounts (401k, IRA) avoids this entirely.
