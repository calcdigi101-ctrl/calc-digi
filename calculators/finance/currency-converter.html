Currency Converter

Convert between major world currencies using reference exchange rates. Useful for travel planning, international purchases, and understanding currency value comparisons.

Important Note on Exchange Rates

Exchange rates fluctuate continuously based on market conditions. The rates in this calculator are reference approximations for planning purposes. For actual transactions, always check live rates from your bank or a financial data provider, as rates can change significantly even within a single day.

Tips for Getting Better Exchange Rates

  • Avoid airport kiosks: They typically offer the worst rates with high fees
  • Use no-foreign-fee cards: Many travel credit cards charge 0% foreign transaction fees
  • Avoid dynamic currency conversion: Always pay in local currency, not your home currency, when given the choice abroad
  • Use bank ATMs: Generally better rates than currency exchange counters
  • Monitor rate trends: If not urgent, exchanging when rates are favorable can save money

Where can I find live exchange rates? +
Financial sites like XE.com, Google Finance, or your bank's website provide live rates. This calculator uses reference rates for planning estimates — always verify current rates before actual transactions.
Why do exchange rates differ between providers? +
Banks and exchange services add a markup (spread) to the mid-market rate as their profit margin. This spread varies by provider — credit unions and online services like Wise often offer better rates than traditional banks.
What is the best way to exchange currency for travel? +
Use a no-foreign-transaction-fee credit card for purchases, and withdraw cash from ATMs (avoiding airport exchanges) for situations requiring cash. Exchange a small amount before travel for immediate needs upon arrival.
