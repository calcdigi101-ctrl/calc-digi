Why Roth IRA Growth is Powerful

Unlike Traditional IRAs (taxed on withdrawal), Roth IRA contributions are made with after-tax dollars. This means every dollar of growth — potentially decades of compound returns — comes out completely tax-free in retirement.

What is the Roth IRA contribution limit? +
$7,000 for under 50, $8,000 for 50+ in 2025. These limits apply across all your IRAs combined (Traditional + Roth), not per account.
Can I withdraw Roth IRA contributions early? +
Yes, you can withdraw your contributions (not earnings) at any time without penalty since you already paid taxes on them. Earnings withdrawn before age 59½ may incur taxes and a 10% penalty unless an exception applies.
Roth IRA vs 401k — which is better? +
Always capture your full employer 401k match first (free money). Beyond that, Roth IRA offers more investment choices and tax-free growth, while Traditional 401k offers an upfront tax deduction. Many people benefit from using both.
