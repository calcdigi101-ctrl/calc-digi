Rent vs Buy Calculator

Compare the true long-term cost of renting versus buying, accounting for opportunity cost, home appreciation, maintenance, and transaction costs. Find your break-even point.

Key Factors in Rent vs Buy

  • Time horizon: Buying typically wins if staying 5+ years due to transaction costs
  • Opportunity cost: Your down payment could otherwise be invested elsewhere
  • Maintenance: Owners pay 1-2% of home value annually for upkeep
  • Appreciation: Home equity grows with both paydown and market appreciation
  • Flexibility: Renting offers easier mobility for career or lifestyle changes

How long should I plan to stay before buying makes sense? +
Generally 5+ years, since closing costs (2-5%) and selling costs (6-10% realtor commission) need time to be offset by equity buildup and appreciation.
Does renting always mean "throwing money away"? +
No. Renting provides housing (a real value) and flexibility. The money saved versus a larger mortgage payment can be invested, potentially building wealth faster than home equity in some markets.
