APY Calculator

Calculate the true Annual Percentage Yield (APY) from a nominal interest rate and compounding frequency. Use this to accurately compare savings accounts, CDs, and money market accounts.

APY Formula

APY = (1 + r/n)^n − 1

Where r = nominal annual interest rate, n = number of compounding periods per year. The more frequently interest compounds, the higher the APY compared to the stated nominal rate.

What is APY vs interest rate? +
The nominal interest rate doesn't account for compounding. APY does, showing your true annual return. A 5% rate compounded daily yields an APY of about 5.13% — higher due to interest earning interest throughout the year.
Why do banks advertise APY instead of interest rate? +
APY gives a true, standardized comparison between accounts regardless of compounding frequency, making it easier (and required by law via Truth in Savings Act) to compare savings products fairly.
