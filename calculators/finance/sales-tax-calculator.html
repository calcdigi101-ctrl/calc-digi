How Sales Tax is Calculated

Tax Amount = Price × Tax Rate
Total Price = Price + Tax Amount = Price × (1 + Tax Rate)

To find the pre-tax price from a total: Pre-tax Price = Total ÷ (1 + Tax Rate)

Which states have no sales tax? +
Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire, and Oregon have no sales tax at all. Alaska has no state sales tax but allows local jurisdictions to charge their own rates.
How do I find my exact local sales tax rate? +
Combined state + local rates vary by city and county. Check your state's Department of Revenue website, or look at a recent receipt, which typically itemizes the exact tax rate applied.
Is sales tax the same as VAT? +
No. Sales tax (used in the US) is applied once at final retail sale. VAT (used in EU and most countries) is applied at each stage of production and distribution, with businesses claiming back VAT paid on inputs.
Are groceries taxed the same as other items? +
No, many states exempt groceries from sales tax or tax them at a reduced rate, since food is considered a necessity. Prepared foods and restaurant meals are usually taxed at the full rate even in these states.
