Home Affordability Calculator

Find out how much house you can realistically afford based on your income, existing debts, and down payment using the standard 28/36 lending rule.

The 28/36 Rule

Max Housing Payment = Gross Monthly Income × 28%
Max Total Debt = Gross Monthly Income × 36%

Lenders use these ratios (called front-end and back-end DTI ratios) to determine how much mortgage you qualify for. Staying below these thresholds, ideally with some buffer, helps ensure manageable payments.

How much house can I afford? +
Using the 28% rule, if you earn $6,000/month, your housing payment should not exceed $1,680. This determines a maximum loan amount based on current interest rates and your down payment.
Should I max out what lenders approve me for? +
Not necessarily. Lenders may approve more than is comfortable for your lifestyle. Many financial advisors recommend staying below the 28% threshold, especially if you have other financial goals like saving for retirement.
