Should I claim Social Security early or wait? +
This depends on health, other income, and life expectancy. Waiting maximizes monthly benefit but requires bridging income until claiming. The break-even point is typically around age 78-80 for delaying vs claiming early.
Is Social Security taxed? +
Up to 85% of benefits can be taxable depending on your "combined income" (AGI + nontaxable interest + half of SS benefits). Lower-income retirees may pay no tax on benefits.
