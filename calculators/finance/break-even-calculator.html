Break Even Calculator

Calculate exactly how many units you need to sell — or how much revenue you need — to cover all your costs and start generating profit. Essential for any new business or product launch.

Break-Even Formula

Break-Even Units = Fixed Costs ÷ (Price per Unit − Variable Cost per Unit)
Contribution Margin = Price − Variable Cost

Every unit sold beyond the break-even point generates pure profit (minus its variable cost), since fixed costs are already covered.

What's the difference between fixed and variable costs? +
Fixed costs stay the same regardless of sales volume (rent, salaries, insurance). Variable costs change with each unit sold (materials, shipping, sales commissions).
How can I lower my break-even point? +
Increase price, reduce variable costs per unit, or reduce fixed costs. Even small improvements in contribution margin significantly lower the units needed to break even.
