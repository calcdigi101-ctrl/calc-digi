Tipping Around the World

Tipping customs vary dramatically internationally. US/Canada: 18-20% expected. UK: 10-15% standard (check for service charge). Europe: round up or 5-10% for good service. Australia/New Zealand: tipping not expected but appreciated. Japan: tipping can be considered rude — exceptional service is expected as standard. Middle East: 10-15% appreciated. Always research local customs before traveling.

Pre-Tax vs Post-Tax Tipping

In the US, the debate: should you tip on the pre-tax subtotal or the post-tax total? Both are acceptable — the difference on a $50 meal with 8% tax is only $0.80 (20% tip: $10.00 pre-tax vs $10.80 post-tax). Most people tip on the post-tax total for simplicity. Either way, tipping based on service quality is more important than the calculation method.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I tip at a restaurant? +
Standard US restaurant tipping: 15% for adequate service, 18-20% for good service, 25%+ for exceptional service. A quick mental calculation for 20%: move the decimal one place left (10%) then double it. On a $47.50 bill: 10% = $4.75, × 2 = $9.50 for 20% tip.
How do I split a restaurant bill with tip? +
Total with tip = Bill × (1 + tip percentage/100). Per person = Total / number of people. Example: $120 bill, 20% tip, 4 people: tip = $24, total = $144, per person = $36. This calculator does all this instantly for any combination.
Should you tip on the pre-tax or post-tax amount? +
Both are acceptable and the difference is small. On a $60 pre-tax bill with 8% tax ($4.80 tax): 20% tip on pre-tax = $12.00. 20% tip on post-tax ($64.80) = $12.96. Difference: $0.96. Most Americans tip on the post-tax total for simplicity. The method matters less than the percentage.
What is a good tip for food delivery? +
Standard delivery tip: 10-15% of order total with a minimum of $3-5. Increase for: long distances, bad weather, large/heavy orders, difficult building access. DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub drivers earn very low base pay — tips represent the majority of their income. A $5 tip on a $20 order (25%) is generous and appreciated.
Do you tip on alcohol at a restaurant? +
Yes, tip on the full bill including alcohol in the US. Alcohol significantly increases bill totals, so tipping the standard percentage is appreciated. For bar tabs with many drinks, $1-2 per drink or 15-20% of the total tab is appropriate. Some diners tip on food only at restaurants where alcohol is expensive, but this can undercompensate servers.
Is it rude to not tip in the US? +
In the US, not tipping (unless service was genuinely poor) is considered very rude and harms service workers who depend on tips for income. If service was poor, 10% is a signal of dissatisfaction — skipping the tip entirely may be taken as forgetting rather than a statement. If you received genuinely bad service, speak to the manager rather than withholding the tip.
