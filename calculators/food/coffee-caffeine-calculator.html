Caffeine Calculator – Daily Intake from Coffee, Tea and Energy Drinks

Calculate your total daily caffeine intake from all sources — coffee, espresso, tea, energy drinks, and soda. Compare to FDA safe limits (400mg/day for adults) and find your caffeine per kilogram of body weight for personalized guidance.

Caffeine is the world most widely consumed psychoactive substance. Understanding your daily intake from all sources — not just coffee — helps you stay within safe limits, avoid afternoon crashes, manage sleep quality, and identify if caffeine dependence is affecting your health.

Safe Caffeine Limits

FDA guidance: up to 400mg caffeine per day is generally safe for healthy adults. This equals approximately 4 cups of coffee. Health Canada: 400mg for most adults, 300mg for women of childbearing age. Pregnancy: limit to 200mg/day maximum. Children and teens: no safe level established — American Academy of Pediatrics discourages caffeine for children under 12. Above 400mg: anxiety, insomnia, elevated heart rate, and headaches common.

Caffeine Half-Life and Sleep

Caffeine half-life in adults is approximately 5-6 hours. A 200mg coffee at 3pm still has ~100mg active in your system at 8pm and ~50mg at 1am — significantly disrupting sleep quality even if you fall asleep normally. For good sleep, most sleep researchers recommend stopping caffeine at least 6-8 hours before bedtime. Genetics affect caffeine metabolism — some people clear it in 3 hours, others take 10+ hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much caffeine is in a cup of coffee? +
Caffeine in an 8 oz (240ml) cup of coffee: drip/brewed: 80-200mg (average ~95mg). Starbucks brewed: ~155-235mg. Dunkin: ~210mg per 14oz. Instant: 27-175mg. Decaf: 2-12mg (not zero). The significant variation comes from coffee bean type, roast level, and brewing method.
How much caffeine is too much per day? +
FDA safe limit: 400mg per day for healthy adults. Signs you may be consuming too much: anxiety, restlessness, insomnia, increased heart rate, headaches, and irritability. For context: 400mg = about 4 average cups of coffee, or 2 large energy drinks (Bang: 300mg per 16oz can). Sensitive individuals may experience effects at much lower doses.
What time should I stop drinking coffee? +
For good sleep quality, stop caffeine 6-8 hours before bedtime. With a 10pm bedtime: last coffee by 2-4pm. Caffeine half-life is ~5-6 hours — a 3pm coffee still has half its caffeine active at 8-9pm. Individual variation is significant: fast metabolizers (CYP1A2 gene) may clear caffeine in 3 hours; slow metabolizers may take 9-10 hours.
Does caffeine affect everyone the same way? +
No. Genetic variation in the CYP1A2 enzyme determines caffeine metabolism speed. Fast metabolizers clear caffeine in 3-4 hours; slow metabolizers in 9-10+ hours. The A2 adenosine receptor gene affects how strongly caffeine blocks adenosine. Tolerance develops with regular use. Age, medications (especially oral contraceptives), liver health, and body weight also affect caffeine response.
How much caffeine is in green tea vs coffee? +
Average 8 oz green tea: 20-50mg caffeine (avg ~28mg). Average 8 oz drip coffee: 80-200mg (avg ~95mg). Coffee has roughly 2-5× more caffeine than green tea. Matcha is higher than regular green tea (60-80mg/8oz) because you consume the whole leaf powder. Earl Grey tea (with bergamot): similar to other black teas, 40-70mg.
What are the symptoms of caffeine overdose? +
Mild overconsumption (400-600mg): anxiety, jitteriness, rapid heartbeat, headache, insomnia. Moderate (600-1000mg): severe anxiety, tremors, GI distress, heart palpitations. Severe (1000mg+): extreme anxiety, panic attacks, dangerous cardiac arrhythmias. Lethal dose: approximately 10,000mg (10g) — nearly impossible from beverages alone (would require ~100 cups of coffee). Energy drink concentrate products pose more realistic overdose risk.
