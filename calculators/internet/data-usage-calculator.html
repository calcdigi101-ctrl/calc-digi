Data Usage Calculator – Monthly Mobile Data Needs

Calculate how much mobile data you use each month based on your streaming, social media, browsing, music, and video call habits. Find the right data plan and avoid expensive overage charges on your mobile bill.

Mobile data plans range from 5 GB to unlimited. Understanding your actual usage helps choose the right plan — overpaying for data you do not use or constantly hitting your cap and paying overage fees. This calculator gives a detailed breakdown by activity.

Formula

Monthly Data = Sum of (Activity Hours × Data Rate per Hour × Days per Month)

How to Reduce Mobile Data Usage

Practical tips to cut mobile data usage: Download content on WiFi for offline use (Spotify, Netflix, YouTube offline). Enable data saving mode in apps (YouTube auto-selects lower quality on mobile data). Turn off auto-play for videos on Facebook and Instagram. Disable automatic app updates on mobile data. Use WiFi calling instead of mobile data for calls.

Choosing the Right Mobile Data Plan

Light user (mainly messaging, some browsing): 5-10 GB/month. Moderate user (social media, some streaming): 15-30 GB/month. Heavy user (daily streaming, video calls, gaming): 50-100 GB/month. Power user (everything, always on): Unlimited plan. Many unlimited plans throttle speeds after 30-75 GB of high-speed data.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much data does YouTube use per hour? +
YouTube data usage: 144p = ~36 MB/hr, 360p = ~320 MB/hr, 720p = ~1.5 GB/hr, 1080p = ~2.5 GB/hr, 4K = ~7-15 GB/hr. YouTube auto-selects quality based on connection and settings. Enable "Data Saver" mode in the app to limit to 360p on mobile data.
How much mobile data do I need per month? +
Light users (messaging, browsing): 5-10 GB. Moderate (social media, occasional streaming): 15-30 GB. Heavy (daily video streaming, video calls): 50+ GB. If you stream 1 hour of HD video daily on mobile: that alone = 30-60 GB/month depending on quality.
Does streaming music use a lot of data? +
Spotify: low quality = 43 MB/hr, normal = 72 MB/hr, high = 144 MB/hr, very high = 288 MB/hr. Apple Music: ~144-288 MB/hr. Streaming music all day (8 hours) at high quality = 1.15 GB — much less than video but significant over a month.
How much data does TikTok use? +
TikTok uses approximately 70-100 MB per 30 minutes of scrolling (140-200 MB/hr). Heavy TikTok use of 2 hours/day = 8-12 GB/month from TikTok alone. Instagram Reels is similar. Download videos on WiFi and watch offline to reduce data usage.
How do I check my mobile data usage? +
iPhone: Settings → Cellular → scroll down to see per-app usage. Android: Settings → Network → Data Usage. Both show usage by app and let you set data warnings and limits. Your carrier app also shows current cycle usage with alerts when approaching limits.
What happens when I exceed my data limit? +
Depending on your carrier: speeds throttled to 2G/3G (very slow, unusable for video), automatic extra data charges ($10-15 per GB), or service suspended until next billing cycle. Most carriers send SMS alerts at 80% and 100% usage. Set alerts in your carrier app to avoid surprises.
