Cloud Storage Calculator – Photos, Videos and Files

Calculate exactly how much cloud storage you need based on your photos, videos, documents, and music collection. Compare storage plans from Google Drive, iCloud, OneDrive, and Dropbox to find the best value for your needs.

Cloud storage needs vary enormously by user type. A casual user with some documents needs 15-50 GB. A photographer shooting RAW files needs hundreds of gigabytes. A videographer working with 4K footage may need several terabytes. Understanding your needs before choosing a plan prevents paying for unused storage.

Formula

Total Storage = Photos × Size per Photo + Videos × Size per Video + Docs + Music + Backup

Photo Storage Requirements

File sizes depend heavily on device and quality settings. iPhone 14 Pro HEIF photo: 2-5 MB. iPhone RAW: 25-35 MB. Android 50MP photo: 8-15 MB. DSLR RAW (24MP): 25-40 MB. DSLR JPEG: 5-10 MB. A photographer shooting 200 RAW photos per session needs 5-8 GB per shoot — 50 shoots per year = 250-400 GB annually.

Video Storage Requirements

Video file sizes vary enormously: 1080p30 iPhone: ~130 MB/min. 4K30 iPhone: ~400 MB/min. 4K60 iPhone: ~800 MB/min. GoPro 4K60: ~1 GB/min. Cinema RAW 6K: 8-15 GB/min. A videographer shooting 1 hour of 4K footage daily generates 24 GB/day — 720 GB/month.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much cloud storage do I need for photos? +
Average smartphone photo: 3-5 MB (HEIF) or 5-15 MB (JPEG). If you take 10 photos/day: 10 × 5 MB × 365 = 18.25 GB/year. For DSLR RAW shooters: 30 MB × 50 photos/session × 50 sessions = 75 GB/year. Plan for 2-3 years of storage minimum.
What is the best free cloud storage? +
Google Drive: 15 GB free (shared with Gmail and Google Photos). Mega: 20 GB free. Dropbox: 2 GB. iCloud: 5 GB. pCloud: 10 GB. For most users, Google One 100 GB ($2.99/month) is the best value paid option. Apple users should prioritize iCloud for seamless device backup.
How long does it take to upload to cloud storage? +
Upload time = File size / Upload speed. At 20 Mbps upload: 100 GB takes 100×1024×8/20 = 40,960 seconds = 11.4 hours. First backup of a large photo library can take days on typical home internet. Use wired ethernet and schedule overnight for large initial uploads.
Should I use Google Drive or iCloud? +
iPhone/Mac users: iCloud integrates seamlessly with the Apple ecosystem — device backup, photo library, iMessage, and document sync all work automatically. Android/Windows users: Google Drive is the better choice with more generous free storage (15 GB vs 5 GB). Many users benefit from both.
Is cloud storage safe for sensitive files? +
Major providers (Google, Microsoft, Apple, Dropbox) encrypt data at rest and in transit. However, providers can technically access your data. For truly sensitive files, use zero-knowledge encryption services (Tresorit, ProtonDrive, Cryptomator) where only you hold the encryption keys. Never store plaintext passwords or unencrypted financial data in standard cloud storage.
How much does 1 TB of cloud storage cost per month? +
Google One 2 TB: $9.99/month ($99.99/year). iCloud+ 2 TB: $9.99/month. OneDrive 1 TB: included with Microsoft 365 ($6.99/month). Dropbox Plus 2 TB: $9.99/month. Backblaze unlimited: $9/month. For 2 TB, Microsoft 365 Personal (includes Office apps + 1 TB OneDrive) is excellent value at $6.99/month.
