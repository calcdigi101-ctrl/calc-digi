The True Cost of Freelancing

Freelancing has hidden costs that employed workers do not face. A freelancer earning $100/hour is NOT equivalent to an employee earning $100/hour. The freelancer must pay both sides of Social Security and Medicare (15.3%), buy their own health insurance, fund their own retirement, and typically works 30-40% of their time on non-billable activities.

Billable vs Non-Billable Hours

Full-time freelancers rarely bill 40 hours per week. Typical breakdown: 25-30 hours billable client work, 5-8 hours administrative (invoicing, bookkeeping, contracts), 3-5 hours marketing and business development, 2-3 hours client communication and proposals. Realistic billable percentage: 60-75% of working hours. New freelancers often overestimate billable hours and set rates too low as a result.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my freelance hourly rate? +
Formula: Hourly Rate = (Annual Income Goal + Annual Business Expenses) / Annual Billable Hours. Example: $80,000 income goal + $15,000 expenses = $95,000 needed. Billable hours: 50 weeks × 30 hours/week = 1,500 hours. Rate: $95,000 / 1,500 = $63.33/hour — but add 15.3% SE tax: divide by 0.847 = $74.77/hour minimum.
Why do freelancers charge more than employees? +
Freelancers charge more because they pay self-employment tax (15.3%), fund their own benefits (health insurance $400-800/month, retirement), have non-billable time (marketing, admin = 25-40% of hours), have no paid vacation or sick leave, face income uncertainty between projects, and must pay for their own equipment and software.
How many billable hours can a freelancer work? +
Realistic annual billable hours: 1,200-1,600 hours (60-80% of a 50-week, 40-hour schedule). 2,000 billable hours/year is technically possible but leads to burnout as it leaves no time for business development, learning, or administration. Plan for 1,300-1,500 billable hours annually for a sustainable freelance business.
What percentage should I save for freelance taxes? +
As a US freelancer: set aside 25-30% of gross income for taxes. Breakdown: self-employment tax 15.3%, federal income tax (varies by bracket), state income tax (0-13%). Make quarterly estimated tax payments by January 15, April 15, June 15, and September 15 to avoid underpayment penalties. Open a separate tax savings account and transfer the percentage with every payment received.
How do I price project work vs hourly? +
Project pricing = Estimated hours × Hourly rate × Risk factor (1.2-1.5×). Add scope risk buffer: undefined or complex projects warrant 30-50% buffer. Benefits of project pricing: clients know total cost upfront, you capture value of working efficiently, rewards expertise. Downside: scope creep risk. Always define deliverables and revision rounds in writing before starting project work.
What is a good freelance rate for web development? +
2024 US freelance web development rates: Junior (1-3 years): $40-75/hour. Mid-level (3-5 years): $75-120/hour. Senior (5+ years): $120-200/hour. Specialist (niche expertise): $150-300+/hour. Offshore rates are significantly lower. Rates vary by technology stack, specialization, and client type (startup vs enterprise). Platform rates (Upwork, Toptal) differ from direct client rates.
