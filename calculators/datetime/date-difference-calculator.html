How Date Difference is Calculated

The difference between two dates is calculated by converting both to timestamps (milliseconds since Jan 1, 1970) and dividing the difference by the number of milliseconds per day.

Days = (Date2 − Date1) ÷ 86,400,000 milliseconds

Working days are calculated by iterating through each day and skipping Saturdays and Sundays. For exact month/year differences, we count forward month by month.

  • Legal contracts: Notice periods, tenancy durations, and warranty expiry dates
  • HR & Payroll: Employee tenure, probation periods, and leave calculations
  • Event planning: Days until wedding, anniversary, graduation, or travel
  • Finance: Loan term lengths, investment holding periods, and interest accrual days

How are working days different from calendar days? +
Calendar days count every day including weekends. Working days (business days) exclude Saturdays and Sundays. A 2-week project starting Monday has 10 working days but 14 calendar days.
Does the calculator include public holidays? +
The working days calculation excludes weekends but does not automatically exclude public holidays, as these vary by country and region. You would need to subtract your local public holidays manually.
Can I calculate dates in the past? +
Yes. You can calculate the difference between any two historical dates — for example, how many days passed between two historical events, or how long ago something happened.
How many days are in a year? +
A standard year has 365 days. A leap year has 366 days. On average, a year is 365.2425 days long, which is why we add a leap day every 4 years to keep the calendar synchronized with Earth's orbit.
