Age Calculator

Calculate your exact age in years, months, days, hours, and minutes. Also shows total days lived and days remaining until your next birthday — all from your date of birth.

How Age is Calculated

Age is not just the difference in years — it depends on whether your birthday has passed this year. Our calculator computes the exact years, remaining months after the last birthday, and remaining days after the last full month.

Age = (Today − Birth Date) broken into years → months → days

For total days lived, we count every calendar day including leap year days (February 29). A person who turns 25 today has lived approximately 9,131 days including leap years.

Why Exact Age Matters

  • Legal milestones: Driving age, voting age, retirement — all depend on the exact date
  • Medical: Drug dosages, vaccine schedules, and health screenings are age-specific
  • Insurance: Premiums often change on the exact birthday, not at year-end
  • Sports: Age-group competitions (Under-18, Under-21) use the exact birthdate cutoff

Fun Age Facts

  • Your heart has beaten approximately age × 42 million times
  • You have taken approximately age × 8 million breaths
  • The average person sleeps about ⅓ of their life — so a 30-year-old has slept ~10 years
  • People born on Feb 29 (leap day) technically only have a birthday every 4 years

How is my exact age calculated? +
We subtract your birth date from today's date step by step — full years first, then remaining months, then remaining days. This accounts for months of different lengths and leap years.
What if I was born on February 29? +
Leap day birthdays are handled correctly. In non-leap years, your birthday is typically recognized on February 28 or March 1 depending on jurisdiction. Our calculator uses Feb 28 for non-leap years.
How many days old am I? +
The total days calculation counts every calendar day from your birth date to today, including all leap year days (Feb 29). Enter your birthdate above to see your exact day count.
How do I calculate someone else's age? +
Simply enter their date of birth in the calculator. You can also change the "as of" date to calculate age at a specific point in time — for example, how old someone was on a particular historical date.
