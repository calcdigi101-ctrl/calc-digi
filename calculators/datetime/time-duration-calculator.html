How to Convert Duration to Decimal Hours

Decimal Hours = H + (M ÷ 60) + (S ÷ 3600)

Example: 2h 30m 45s = 2 + (30÷60) + (45÷3600) = 2 + 0.5 + 0.0125 = 2.5125 decimal hours

Decimal hours are essential for payroll and billing — multiply decimal hours by hourly rate to get exact pay.
