Leap Year Rules

Leap Year: divisible by 4, EXCEPT century years must be divisible by 400

  • 2025 → not divisible by 4 ✗ → Not a leap year (365 days)
  • 1900 → divisible by 4, but century year not divisible by 400 ✗ → Not a leap year
  • 2000 → century year divisible by 400 ✓ → Leap year

When is the next leap year? +
After 2024, the next leap years are 2028, 2032, 2036, and 2040. They occur every 4 years except century years not divisible by 400.
Why do we have leap years? +
Earth's orbit takes approximately 365.2425 days. Without leap years, our calendar would drift about 6 hours per year — after 100 years, seasons would be off by 24 days.
What year will it be in 10 years? +
Enter today's date and 10 years in the Year Calculator to find the exact date 10 years from now, including whether that year is a leap year.
How many weeks are in a year? +
A standard year has 52 weeks and 1 day. A leap year has 52 weeks and 2 days. ISO week numbering means a year can have 52 or 53 calendar weeks depending on which day January 1 falls on.
