What is the most common birthday? +
September 9 is statistically the most common birthday in the US, followed by other mid-September dates. This is because September falls about 9 months after the winter holiday season in December.
What is a golden birthday? +
A golden birthday happens when you turn the age equal to your birth date number. For example, turning 22 on the 22nd of the month. It is considered a once-in-a-lifetime special birthday.
What is a numerology life path number? +
Add all digits of your full birth date repeatedly until you get a single digit (1–9) or master numbers 11, 22, 33. For example: 15/06/1990 → 1+5+0+6+1+9+9+0 = 31 → 3+1 = 4. Life path 4 represents stability and hard work.
What zodiac sign am I if born on March 21? +
March 21 is the first day of Aries (♈), the first sign of the zodiac. Aries runs from March 21 to April 19 and is represented by the ram.
