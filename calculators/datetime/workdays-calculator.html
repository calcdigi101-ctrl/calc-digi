Working Days Calculator

Add or subtract business days from any start date — automatically skipping weekends. Perfect for calculating delivery dates, project deadlines, notice periods, and legal timelines.

How Business Day Calculation Works

Start from date → skip Saturdays & Sundays → count N working days forward/backward

To find the date 10 business days from Monday Jan 6: count Mon–Fri of week 1 (5 days), then Mon–Fri of week 2 (5 more days) = Jan 17. Weekends are automatically skipped.

  • Notice periods: Employment contracts often require 30 business days notice

What is 10 business days from today? +
Enter today's date and 10 business days in the calculator. The result depends on which day of the week today falls on — weekends will be skipped automatically.
Does this include public holidays? +
This calculator excludes weekends only. Public holidays vary by country and region, so you would need to manually add extra days for any holidays that fall within your working day range.
How many business days are in a year? +
A standard year has 260–262 business days (52 weeks × 5 working days). After subtracting public holidays (typically 8–11 in the US), it is roughly 249–253 working days.
Can I subtract business days? +
Yes. Enter a negative number (e.g. -10) or use the subtract mode to find a date that is N business days in the past. This is useful for finding when a process must have started.
