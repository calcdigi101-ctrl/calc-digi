Time Calculator

Add or subtract time values in hours, minutes, and seconds. Also calculate the exact duration between any two times — including overnight spans that cross midnight.

How Time Addition Works

Total seconds = H×3600 + M×60 + S → convert back to H:M:S

To add 2h 45m + 1h 30m: convert to 165 + 90 = 255 minutes = 4h 15m. When seconds exceed 60, carry over to minutes. When minutes exceed 60, carry over to hours.

Common Uses

  • Work hours: Add up daily work sessions to get total hours worked
  • Sports timing: Calculate lap times, race durations, and training sessions
  • Video/audio: Add clip durations to calculate total project length
  • Shift planning: Calculate shift lengths and schedule gaps

How do I add multiple time values? +
Convert each time value to total seconds, sum them all, then convert back to hours, minutes, and seconds. Our calculator does this automatically for two time values.
How does overnight time calculation work? +
If the end time is earlier than start time (e.g., 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM), we add 24 hours to the end time before subtracting. This gives the correct 8-hour duration across midnight.
How do I convert decimal hours to hours and minutes? +
Multiply the decimal part by 60. For example: 2.75 hours = 2 hours + 0.75×60 = 2 hours 45 minutes.
What is the difference between AM and PM? +
AM (ante meridiem) is midnight to noon (00:00–11:59). PM (post meridiem) is noon to midnight (12:00–23:59). In 24-hour format, just add 12 to PM hours: 3 PM = 15:00.
