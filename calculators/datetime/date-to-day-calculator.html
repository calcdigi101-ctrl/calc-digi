How Day of Week is Calculated

The day of the week for any date is calculated using Zeller's congruence or the Tomohiko Sakamoto algorithm. JavaScript's built-in Date object handles this automatically and accurately for all dates from year 1 onward.

dayOfWeek = new Date(year, month-1, day).getDay() → 0=Sun, 1=Mon ... 6=Sat

This works for any date — ancient history, today, or far into the future — because it uses the proleptic Gregorian calendar.
