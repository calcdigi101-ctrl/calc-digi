TDEE Calculator

Your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) is the total number of calories you burn each day. Knowing your TDEE is the first step to any successful diet or fitness plan.

What Is TDEE?

TDEE stands for Total Daily Energy Expenditure — the total number of calories your body burns in a 24-hour period, including your resting metabolism (BMR) and all physical activity.

If you eat exactly your TDEE, your weight stays the same. Eat less to lose weight, eat more to gain.

TDEE = BMR × Activity Factor

Sedentary: BMR × 1.2 | Light: BMR × 1.375 | Moderate: BMR × 1.55
Very Active: BMR × 1.725 | Extra Active: BMR × 1.9

How do I use my TDEE to lose weight? +
Subtract 300–500 calories from your TDEE to create a healthy calorie deficit. This should produce 0.5–1 lb of weight loss per week without feeling deprived.
Does TDEE change over time? +
Yes. As you lose weight, your BMR decreases because there's less body mass to maintain. Recalculate your TDEE every 10–15 lbs of weight change or every 2–3 months.
Why is TDEE different from BMR? +
BMR is the calories you'd burn lying still all day. TDEE adds the calories burned from all daily movement and exercise. Most people's TDEE is 1.2–1.9× their BMR.
