Waist to Height Ratio Calculator

Calculate your Waist-to-Height Ratio (WHtR) — one of the most powerful and simple screening tools for cardiometabolic health risk. A single threshold of 0.5 applies to both men and women of all ages and ethnicities.

What is Waist-to-Height Ratio?

WHtR is calculated by dividing waist circumference by height. Unlike BMI, it directly measures abdominal fat relative to body size, making it a better predictor of cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes, and metabolic syndrome.

WHtR = Waist Circumference ÷ Height (same units)

The simple public health message: "Keep your waist to less than half your height."

For a person 5'8" (68 inches) tall, a healthy waist is under 34 inches. For someone 6'0" (72 inches), under 36 inches. This intuitive rule works regardless of sex, age, or ethnicity.

What is a healthy waist to height ratio? +
A WHtR below 0.5 is considered healthy for adults of both sexes. Simply put: your waist should be less than half your height. This threshold has been validated across multiple ethnicities and age groups in large-scale research.
Is WHtR better than BMI for health screening? +
Many researchers believe so. WHtR better predicts cardiometabolic risk because it specifically measures abdominal fat proportion relative to body size. BMI cannot distinguish between muscle and fat or between dangerous abdominal fat and less harmful fat elsewhere.
How do I reduce my waist circumference? +
A combination of caloric deficit (300-500 calories/day below TDEE), aerobic exercise (150+ min/week), and resistance training is most effective. Reducing refined carbohydrates and added sugars specifically targets visceral fat. Sleep and stress management also significantly affect abdominal fat storage.
Where do I measure my waist for this calculator? +
Measure at the midpoint between the lowest rib and the top of the hip bone — approximately at or just above the belly button. Breathe out normally before measuring. Do not suck in your stomach. Keep the tape snug but not compressing the skin.
