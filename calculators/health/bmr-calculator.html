BMR Calculator

Your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) is the number of calories your body burns at complete rest. It's the foundation of every nutrition plan — know yours before setting any diet goals.

What Is BMR?

BMR represents the minimum energy your body needs to perform basic life-sustaining functions: breathing, circulation, cell repair, and temperature regulation. It accounts for roughly 60-75% of your total daily calorie burn.

Mifflin-St Jeor (Male): BMR = (10 x kg) + (6.25 x cm) - (5 x age) + 5
Mifflin-St Jeor (Female): BMR = (10 x kg) + (6.25 x cm) - (5 x age) - 161

Should I eat below my BMR to lose weight? +
No. Eating below your BMR long-term can slow your metabolism, cause muscle loss, and lead to nutrient deficiencies. Use your TDEE (BMR x activity level) as your baseline, then create a moderate deficit of 300-500 calories.
What increases BMR naturally? +
Building lean muscle mass is the most effective way to increase your BMR long-term. Adequate sleep, staying hydrated, and eating enough protein also help maintain a healthy metabolic rate.
