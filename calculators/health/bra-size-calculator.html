How to Measure Your Bra Size

Getting an accurate bra size requires two simple measurements taken with a soft measuring tape:

Step 1 — Band Size (Underbust)

Wrap the tape measure around your ribcage directly under your bust, keeping it snug and level. Breathe out normally and note the measurement in inches. If the result is an odd number, round up to the next even number.

Step 2 — Bust Size (Fullest Point)

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape parallel to the ground and slightly loose. Do not compress the breast tissue.

Step 3 — Calculate Cup Size

Subtract your band size from your bust measurement. The difference determines your cup size:

Signs Your Bra Does Not Fit Correctly

  • Band rides up: Band is too large — go down a band size
  • Straps digging in: Band is too loose, straps compensating — go down a band size
  • Cup overflows: Cup is too small — go up a cup size
  • Cup gapes or wrinkles: Cup is too large — go down a cup size
  • Center wire pokes: Cup is too small — go up a cup size
  • Wire digs under arms: Cup is too small — try a wider wire style or larger cup

Studies show that up to 80% of women wear the wrong bra size. An ill-fitting bra can cause back pain, shoulder pain, skin irritation, and poor posture.

How do I measure my bra size at home? +
You need a soft measuring tape. Measure your underbust (band size) and your fullest bust point. Subtract band from bust to get the cup size difference. Our calculator does the math automatically.
What is a sister size? +
Sister sizes share the same cup volume but have different band sizes. Going up one band size means going down one cup size (e.g., 34C → 36B). This is useful when the correct size is unavailable or the band fit needs adjustment.
Why does my bra size feel different in different brands? +
Bra sizing is not standardized globally. Construction methods, fabric stretch, and wire placement vary significantly between brands. Always try bras on when possible, and use brand-specific size guides for online shopping.
How often should I get re-measured? +
Re-measure every 6-12 months, or after significant weight changes, pregnancy, breastfeeding, or if your current bras feel uncomfortable. Breast tissue changes throughout life due to hormones, age, and body composition shifts.
