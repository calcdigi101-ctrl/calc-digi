Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories do I need to cut to lose 1 lb per week? +
One pound of fat equals approximately 3,500 calories. To lose 1 lb per week, you need a daily calorie deficit of 500 calories through diet, exercise, or a combination of both.
Is it safe to lose more than 2 lbs per week? +
Losing more than 1–2 lbs per week for most people increases the risk of muscle loss, nutrient deficiencies, and metabolic adaptation. Consult your doctor before pursuing aggressive weight loss.
What is a healthy rate of weight loss? +
The CDC and most health organizations recommend losing 1–2 lbs per week as a safe, sustainable pace. This typically requires a 500–1,000 calorie per day deficit.
