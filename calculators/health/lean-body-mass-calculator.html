Frequently Asked Questions

What is lean body mass? +
Lean body mass (LBM) is the total weight of your body minus your fat mass. It includes muscles, bones, organs, blood, and water. LBM is important for calculating accurate calorie and protein needs.
How is lean body mass different from muscle mass? +
Lean body mass includes muscle, bone, organs, and water — everything except fat. Muscle mass refers only to skeletal muscle tissue, which is a subset of LBM.
Why is lean body mass important? +
LBM determines your resting metabolic rate, informs safe protein intake recommendations, and helps track the effectiveness of fitness programs by separating fat loss from muscle gain.
