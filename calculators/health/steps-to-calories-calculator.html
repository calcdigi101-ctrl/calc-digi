How Steps Convert to Calories

The number of calories you burn per step depends on two main factors: your body weight (heavier people burn more) and your stride length (which determines how far each step takes you).

Stride Length ≈ Height × 0.414 (walking)
Distance = Steps × Stride Length
Calories = MET × Weight (kg) × Distance (km) × 1.036

Walking pace affects the MET value used: slow walking (2 mph) = MET 2.8, moderate (3 mph) = MET 3.5, brisk (3.5 mph) = MET 4.3, fast (4+ mph) = MET 5.0.

How many calories does 10,000 steps burn? +
A 155-pound person burns approximately 400-500 calories walking 10,000 steps (about 5 miles). The exact amount depends on your weight, height, and pace. Heavier people and brisker walkers burn more.
How many steps equal a mile? +
On average, 2,000-2,500 steps equal one mile. Taller people with longer strides take fewer steps per mile. A 5'4" person takes about 2,400 steps per mile, while a 6'0" person takes about 2,000.
Is 10,000 steps a good daily goal? +
10,000 steps is a popular goal but research shows health benefits start at 7,000-8,000 steps/day. More is generally better. Even increasing from 2,000 to 5,000 steps daily significantly improves health markers.
Do steps count on a treadmill? +
Yes! Treadmill steps count the same as outdoor steps. In fact, treadmill walking on an incline burns more calories per step than flat ground walking, making it a great option for burning extra calories.
