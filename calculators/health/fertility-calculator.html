Understanding Your Fertility Window

Conception can only occur when a mature egg is present — and a human egg survives only 12-24 hours after ovulation. However, sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for 3-5 days, which creates a fertile window of approximately 6 days per cycle.

The highest pregnancy probability comes from intercourse 1-2 days before ovulation — not on ovulation day itself. This is because timing allows sperm to already be present and waiting when the egg arrives.

Ovulation Day = Cycle Length − Luteal Phase (typically 14 days)
Peak Fertile Days = Ovulation Day − 1 to Ovulation Day − 2

Factors That Affect Fertility

  • Age: Female fertility declines significantly after 35; male fertility declines more gradually
  • BMI: Both underweight and overweight can disrupt ovulation and hormone levels
  • Smoking: Reduces egg quality and quantity; increases miscarriage risk
  • Alcohol: Heavy consumption reduces fertility in both men and women
  • Stress: Chronic stress can suppress ovulation and reduce sperm quality
  • Thyroid function: Both hypo and hyperthyroidism affect fertility significantly
  • PCOS: Affects up to 10% of women and is a leading cause of infertility

When am I most fertile? +
Your peak fertility is in the 1-2 days before ovulation. Ovulation typically occurs 14 days before your next period. Our calculator identifies these peak days so you can time intercourse for the highest probability of conception.
How long does it take to get pregnant? +
About 85% of healthy couples conceive within 12 months of trying. If you are under 35 and have been trying for 12+ months without success, or over 35 and trying for 6+ months, consult a reproductive endocrinologist.
Can I get pregnant if we have sex on ovulation day? +
Yes, but the probability is slightly lower (~25%) than the day before ovulation (~31%). The egg only survives 12-24 hours, so timing intercourse 1-2 days before gives sperm time to be present when the egg is released.
What supplements help with fertility? +
Folic acid (400-800 mcg) is essential for preventing neural tube defects and should be started 3 months before trying to conceive. CoQ10 may improve egg quality. Vitamin D, zinc, and omega-3s also support reproductive health. Always consult your doctor before starting supplements.
