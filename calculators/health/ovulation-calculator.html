How Ovulation Works

Ovulation is the release of a mature egg from the ovary. It typically occurs once per menstrual cycle, about 14 days before your next expected period — regardless of how long your cycle is.

The fertile window spans approximately 6 days: the 5 days before ovulation and the day of ovulation itself. Sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to 5 days, so having intercourse before ovulation can still result in conception.

Signs of Ovulation

  • Cervical mucus changes: Becomes clear, stretchy, and egg-white like
  • Basal body temperature (BBT) rise: Slight increase of 0.2–0.5°F after ovulation
  • Mild pelvic pain: Called mittelschmerz — a twinge or cramp on one side
  • Breast tenderness: Mild sensitivity around ovulation time

When do I ovulate in my cycle? +
Ovulation typically occurs 14 days before your next period starts. For a 28-day cycle, that is day 14. For a 32-day cycle, it would be around day 18.
How long is the fertility window? +
The fertile window is about 6 days — the 5 days before ovulation and the day of ovulation. The 2 days just before ovulation and ovulation day itself have the highest pregnancy probability.
Can I get pregnant outside the fertility window? +
It is very unlikely but not impossible, especially if your cycle is irregular. Ovulation can sometimes be earlier or later than expected due to stress, illness, or hormonal changes.
Is this calculator accurate for irregular cycles? +
This calculator is most accurate for regular cycles. If your cycle varies by more than 7 days, consider tracking basal body temperature or using ovulation predictor kits for more precision.
How many days after my period do I ovulate? +
For a typical 28-day cycle, ovulation occurs around day 14 (14 days after the first day of your period). For a 30-day cycle, around day 16. Use the calculator above for your specific cycle length.
