Frequently Asked Questions

How much water should I drink per day? +
The general guideline from the National Academies of Sciences is about 125 oz (about 15.5 cups) for men and 91 oz (about 11.5 cups) for women per day from all beverages and food. Your personal needs vary by weight, activity, and climate.
Does coffee count toward daily water intake? +
Yes. Despite being mildly diuretic in high quantities, coffee and tea do count toward your daily fluid intake for most people who consume them in moderation.
Should I drink more water when exercising? +
Yes. Aim to drink an additional 12–24 oz of water per hour of moderate exercise. If you sweat heavily, you may need even more along with electrolytes.
