Frequently Asked Questions

How much protein do I need per day? +
The RDA for protein is 0.36 grams per pound of body weight (0.8 g/kg). However, active individuals, athletes, and those trying to build muscle typically need 0.7–1.0 g/lb (1.6–2.2 g/kg) per day for optimal results.
Can I eat too much protein? +
For healthy adults, consuming up to 2.0 g/lb per day is generally considered safe. Very high intakes may stress the kidneys in people with pre-existing kidney conditions. Consult your doctor if you have concerns.
What are the best protein sources? +
Lean meats (chicken, turkey, beef), fish, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, legumes, tofu, and protein supplements are all excellent sources of high-quality protein.
