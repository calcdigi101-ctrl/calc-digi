Pregnancy Due Date Calculator

Calculate your estimated due date (EDD), current pregnancy week, trimester, and key milestone dates. Based on Naegele's Rule — the standard used by OB-GYNs across the USA.

How to Calculate Your Due Date

The most common method for calculating a due date is Naegele's Rule, developed in the 19th century and still used today. It works by adding 280 days (40 weeks) to the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP).

If you know your conception date, we add 266 days (38 weeks) instead, since conception typically occurs about 14 days after your LMP.

Due Date = Last Menstrual Period + 280 days (40 weeks)

For example, if your LMP was January 1st, your estimated due date would be October 8th of the same year.

Important Pregnancy Milestones

  • Week 6–8: First heartbeat detectable on ultrasound
  • Week 10–13: First trimester screening and NT scan
  • Week 18–20: Anatomy scan (gender may be revealed)
  • Week 24: Viability milestone — baby can survive outside womb with support
  • Week 28: Third trimester begins, glucose screening
  • Week 36–37: Baby considered early term
  • Week 39–40: Full term — ideal delivery window

How is my due date calculated? +
We use Naegele's Rule: add 280 days (40 weeks) to the first day of your last menstrual period. This is the standard method used by healthcare providers in the USA.
How accurate is an online due date calculator? +
Online calculators give an estimated due date. Only about 5% of babies are born on their exact due date. Ultrasound dating, especially in the first trimester, is the most accurate method.
What week is considered full term? +
Full term is 39–40 weeks. Early term is 37–38 weeks. Late term is 41 weeks. Post-term is 42+ weeks and may require medical intervention.
What if my cycle is not 28 days? +
Naegele's Rule assumes a 28-day cycle. If your cycle is longer or shorter, your actual due date may differ slightly. Use the conception date option for more accuracy, or ask your doctor for an ultrasound date.
Can I use this calculator for IVF pregnancies? +
For IVF, your fertility clinic will provide a precise due date based on your embryo transfer date. Our conception date option can be used as a rough guide, but always defer to your clinic's calculation.
