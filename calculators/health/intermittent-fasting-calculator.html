Benefits of Intermittent Fasting

  • Weight loss: Reduces calorie intake naturally and shifts body to fat burning
  • Insulin sensitivity: Lowers fasting insulin levels, reducing type 2 diabetes risk
  • Autophagy: Cellular cleanup process activated during extended fasting
  • Mental clarity: Many people report improved focus during fasting windows
  • Simplified eating: Fewer meals means less meal planning and preparation
  • Reduced inflammation: Studies show IF reduces inflammatory markers

What Breaks a Fast?

Does NOT break your fast: Water, black coffee, plain tea, sparkling water, electrolytes (no calories), medications (consult doctor)

DOES break your fast: Any food, milk/cream in coffee, fruit juice, diet sodas (debated), protein shakes, bone broth with significant calories, gum with calories

What is the best IF protocol for beginners? +
16:8 is ideal for beginners. Most people simply skip breakfast and eat from noon to 8 PM. It aligns with natural hunger patterns and requires minimal lifestyle adjustment. Start with 14:10 if 16 hours feels too long.
Can I drink coffee during fasting? +
Yes — black coffee with no milk, cream, or sweeteners does not break a fast. It may actually enhance fasting benefits by suppressing appetite and boosting fat oxidation. Limit to 1-2 cups to avoid excess cortisol.
Is intermittent fasting safe? +
IF is safe for most healthy adults. It is not recommended for pregnant or breastfeeding women, people with a history of eating disorders, those with type 1 diabetes, or anyone underweight. Always consult a doctor before starting.
Will I lose muscle on intermittent fasting? +
Short-term IF does not significantly increase muscle loss compared to continuous calorie restriction, especially if protein intake is adequate and you resistance train. Eating sufficient protein during your eating window is key to preserving muscle.
How many calories should I eat during my eating window? +
Your total daily calorie goal should be the same as without IF — just consumed in a shorter window. Use our Calorie Calculator or TDEE Calculator to find your target, then subtract 300-500 calories for weight loss.
