The 5 Body Shapes Explained

Apple

🍐

What are the 5 body shapes? +
Apple (weight around middle), Pear (wider hips), Hourglass (equal bust and hips with small waist), Rectangle (similar measurements throughout), and Inverted Triangle (wider shoulders/bust than hips). Each has different health profiles and exercise recommendations.
Is apple or pear body shape healthier? +
Pear shapes are generally considered healthier from a cardiovascular standpoint. Apple shapes accumulate visceral (abdominal) fat, which is metabolically active and associated with higher risk of heart disease, diabetes, and metabolic syndrome. Pear-shaped fat is subcutaneous and less harmful.
Can I change my body shape? +
Your skeletal structure determines your base shape, but body composition can be significantly changed. Targeted strength training builds muscle in specific areas, and fat loss reduces overall deposits. You cannot spot-reduce fat, but you can reshape your body through consistent training and nutrition.
How accurate is a body shape calculator? +
Body shape calculators provide a general classification based on your proportions. They are a useful starting point for understanding your fat distribution patterns and health risks, but individual variation means two people with the same classification can look and function quite differently.
