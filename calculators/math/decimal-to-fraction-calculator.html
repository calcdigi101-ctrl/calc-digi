How to Convert Decimal to Fraction

0.75 → 75/100 → GCF(75,100)=25 → 3/4
  1. Count the decimal places (0.75 has 2 decimal places)
  2. Multiply by 10^n to remove decimal: 0.75 × 100 = 75
  3. Write as fraction: 75/100
  4. Find GCF(75,100) = 25
  5. Divide both by GCF: 75/100 = 3/4 ✓

How do you convert 0.75 to a fraction? +
0.75 = 75/100. Find GCF(75,100) = 25. Divide both by 25: 3/4. So 0.75 = 3/4.
Can all decimals become fractions? +
Terminating decimals (like 0.75) and repeating decimals (like 0.333…) always convert to exact fractions. Irrational numbers (like π = 3.14159…) cannot be expressed as exact fractions.
What is a mixed number vs improper fraction? +
An improper fraction has a numerator larger than its denominator (e.g., 7/4). A mixed number separates the whole part from the fractional part (e.g., 1¾). Both represent the same value.
How do you simplify a fraction? +
Find the GCF of the numerator and denominator, then divide both by it. Repeat until no common factor exists. The result is the fraction in its simplest (lowest) terms.
