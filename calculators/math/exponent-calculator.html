Exponent Rules

x^n = x × x × x ... (n times)
  • Negative exponent: x^-n = 1/x^n
  • Zero exponent: x^0 = 1 (for any nonzero x)
  • Fractional exponent: x^(1/n) = nth root of x
  • Product rule: x^a × x^b = x^(a+b)
  • Power rule: (x^a)^b = x^(a×b)

What does a negative exponent mean? +
It means take the reciprocal: x^-n = 1/x^n. For example, 5^-2 = 1/25 = 0.04. The base flips to the denominator while the exponent becomes positive.
What is anything to the power of 0? +
Any nonzero number to the power of 0 equals 1. This includes negative numbers and fractions: (-5)^0 = 1, (1/2)^0 = 1. 0^0 is mathematically undefined/indeterminate.
How do fractional exponents work? +
x^(m/n) means take the nth root of x, then raise to the m power (or vice versa): x^(2/3) = (³√x)². This connects exponents directly to roots.
