What is a Square Root?

The square root of a number x is a value that, when multiplied by itself, gives x. It is written as √x or x^(1/2).

√x × √x = x

For example, √25 = 5 because 5 × 5 = 25. Square roots of perfect squares (1, 4, 9, 16, 25...) are whole numbers, while most other numbers have irrational square roots.

How do I calculate square root by hand? +
Newton's method works well: start with a guess, then repeatedly compute (guess + x/guess)/2 until the value stabilizes. For example, to find √10, guess 3: (3+10/3)/2 = 3.1667, then repeat for more precision.
What is the square root of a negative number? +
It is not a real number — it's an imaginary number expressed with i, where i² = -1. The square root of -9 is 3i. Real square roots only exist for non-negative numbers.
What is a perfect square? +
A perfect square is the result of squaring a whole number: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36... Their square roots are exact whole numbers, unlike most other numbers which have irrational (non-terminating) square roots.
