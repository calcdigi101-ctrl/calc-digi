How do you calculate area of a circle? +
Area = π × r², where r is the radius (half the diameter). A circle with a 10-unit diameter has radius 5, giving area ≈ 78.54 square units.
What units does area use? +
Area is always in square units of whatever length unit you use — square feet, square meters, square inches, etc. If inputs are in feet, the area result is in square feet.
What is the formula for a trapezoid's area? +
Area = ½ × (base1 + base2) × height, averaging the two parallel sides and multiplying by the perpendicular height between them.
How is a rectangle's area different from its perimeter? +
Area measures the space inside (length × width, in square units). Perimeter measures the distance around the edge (2 × (length + width), in linear units). They serve different purposes.
