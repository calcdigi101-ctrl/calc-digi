Division Fundamentals

What is a remainder in division? +
The remainder is the amount left over after dividing as many whole times as possible. For 23÷7: 7 goes in 3 times (7×3=21), leaving 23-21=2 as the remainder.
Can you divide by zero? +
No, division by zero is mathematically undefined. No number multiplied by zero gives a nonzero result.
How do I convert a remainder to a decimal? +
Divide the remainder by the divisor and add to the whole quotient. For 17÷5: quotient=3, remainder=2, decimal = 3 + 2/5 = 3.4.
What is the modulo operation? +
Modulo (mod) is the remainder after division. In programming, 17 mod 5 = 2. It's widely used in cryptography, clock arithmetic, and computing.
