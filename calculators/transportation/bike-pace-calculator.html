Cycling Pace Calculator – Speed, Distance and Ride Time

Calculate your cycling speed, time to complete any distance, or distance covered at a given pace. Plan your bike rides, track your training progress, and compare your cycling performance to average speeds for different cyclist levels.

Whether you are a casual cyclist, commuter, or competitive road racer, understanding your cycling pace helps you plan rides, track improvement, and set realistic goals. This calculator handles all three scenarios — finding speed from distance and time, finding time from speed and distance, or finding distance from speed and time.

Formula

Speed (mph) = Distance / Time | Pace (min/mile) = 60 / Speed (mph) | Distance = Speed × Time

Factors Affecting Cycling Speed

Wind is the biggest external factor — a 15 mph headwind can reduce speed by 5-8 mph and increase effort dramatically. Gradient matters significantly: a 5% climb reduces speed by 30-40% for the same power output. Bike type: road bike (fastest, most aerodynamic), gravel bike (5-10% slower), hybrid (10-15% slower), mountain bike (20-30% slower on pavement), and cargo bike (20-40% slower).

Calories Burned Cycling

Cycling calorie burn: leisure (10 mph): ~300-400 cal/hour. Moderate (14 mph): ~450-600 cal/hour. Vigorous (18 mph): ~600-900 cal/hour. These are approximate for a 155 lb (70 kg) cyclist. Heavier cyclists burn more; tailwinds burn less. Climbing burns significantly more calories than flat riding at the same speed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good cycling pace for a beginner? +
Beginners typically average 8-12 mph on flat terrain. A 12 mph pace means riding 1 mile every 5 minutes. For comparison, walking speed is 3-4 mph and jogging is 5-7 mph. Most beginners find 30-60 minute rides at 10-12 mph comfortable. Speed improves significantly with consistent training over 2-3 months.
How long does it take to cycle 20 miles? +
At 12 mph: 20/12 = 1.67 hours = 1 hour 40 minutes. At 15 mph: 1 hour 20 minutes. At 18 mph: 1 hour 7 minutes. These are moving time only — add time for stops, traffic lights, and rest breaks. A 20-mile casual ride with stops typically takes 2-2.5 hours door to door.
What is a good cycling pace per mile? +
In minutes per mile: Beginner: 5-7.5 min/mile (8-12 mph). Intermediate: 3.75-5 min/mile (12-16 mph). Advanced: 3-3.75 min/mile (16-20 mph). Elite: 2.5-3 min/mile (20-24 mph). In minutes per km: Beginner: 3.1-4.7 min/km. Intermediate: 2.3-3.1 min/km.
How many calories do you burn cycling 30 minutes? +
Cycling 30 minutes at moderate pace (14 mph): approximately 225-350 calories for a 155 lb (70 kg) person. At vigorous pace (18 mph): 350-450 calories. Leisurely pace (10 mph): 150-225 calories. Cycling is one of the most calorie-efficient cardio exercises due to the mechanical advantage of the bike.
How fast do pro cyclists ride in the Tour de France? +
Tour de France average stage speed: 24-27 mph (38-43 km/h). Time trial stages: 30-35 mph average. Mountain stages: 15-20 mph due to climbing. The fastest stage speeds exceed 35 mph. Stage total distances: 100-220 km. Over 21 stages and 3,500+ km, it remains the most grueling annual sporting event.
Is cycling 10 miles a day good exercise? +
10 miles at 12 mph = 50 minutes of moderate cardio — exceeding the WHO recommendation of 150 minutes of moderate activity per week (you achieve this in 3 days of cycling). A 150 lb person burns approximately 400-500 calories cycling 10 miles at moderate pace. Cycling commuting (10 miles each way) provides 100 minutes of daily exercise.
