CGPA Calculator – Cumulative GPA Across Semesters

Calculate your Cumulative GPA (CGPA) by combining multiple semesters of academic results. Convert CGPA to percentage, find your overall academic standing, and plan the grades needed in future semesters to reach your target CGPA.

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the overall GPA calculated across all semesters of your academic program. Used widely in India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, and other countries following the 10-point grading scale, CGPA gives a comprehensive view of academic performance across the entire degree program.

Formula

CGPA = Total Grade Points Earned / Total Credit Hours | Percentage = CGPA × 9.5 (Indian system) | GPA (4.0) = CGPA × 0.4

CGPA Calculation Guide

CGPA is the weighted average of GPA across all completed semesters. Each semester GPA is weighted by its credit hours — a semester with 24 credit hours counts more than a semester with 15 credit hours. This ensures that heavier academic loads are proportionally represented in the cumulative average.

Converting CGPA to Percentage (Indian System)

The official UGC (University Grants Commission) India formula: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5. A CGPA of 8.0 on a 10-point scale = 76%. CGPA of 9.0 = 85.5%. CGPA of 7.5 = 71.25%. Different universities may use slightly different multipliers (some use 9.5, some use 10). Always check your university official conversion formula.

CGPA and Employment

Many Indian companies have minimum CGPA cutoffs for campus placements: IT companies (TCS, Infosys, Wipro): 6.0-7.0 CGPA minimum. Product companies (Google, Microsoft, Amazon): 7.5+ preferred. Government jobs: varies by department. CGPA cutoffs are often used as initial filters before technical interviews — maintaining above the cutoff is essential for eligibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is CGPA calculated? +
CGPA = Total Grade Points / Total Credits across all semesters. If Semester 1: GPA 8.5 × 24 credits = 204, Semester 2: GPA 7.8 × 20 credits = 156, Semester 3: GPA 8.2 × 22 credits = 180.4. Total: 540.4 / 66 credits = CGPA 8.19.
How do I convert CGPA to percentage? +
Indian UGC formula: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5. CGPA 8.0 = 76%, CGPA 7.5 = 71.25%, CGPA 9.0 = 85.5%. Some universities use their own multipliers. VIT uses ×9.5, Anna University uses direct percentage grading. Always check your institution specific conversion policy.
What CGPA do I need for good job prospects in India? +
Top IT companies: 6.5-7.5 CGPA minimum for eligibility. Core engineering companies: 6.5-8.0. Management consulting: 7.5+ preferred. PSU (Public Sector Undertaking): varies, typically 6.5-7.5 with GATE scores. Startups: less focus on CGPA, more on skills and projects.
Can I convert CGPA to 4.0 GPA? +
Approximate conversion: US GPA = (CGPA / 10) × 4. CGPA 8.0/10 ≈ 3.2/4.0. CGPA 9.0/10 ≈ 3.6/4.0. CGPA 7.5/10 ≈ 3.0/4.0. For graduate school applications, universities often request a detailed transcript and convert grades themselves rather than relying on student-calculated conversions.
How do I raise my CGPA significantly? +
Raising CGPA becomes harder as semesters accumulate because each new semester has less mathematical impact. In early semesters: maximize GPA to set a high baseline. Strategy for later semesters: calculate exactly what GPA each future semester needs. Retaking courses (where allowed) to replace poor grades can significantly raise CGPA.
What is the difference between GPA and CGPA? +
GPA (Grade Point Average) is calculated for a single semester or term. CGPA (Cumulative GPA) is the weighted average across all completed semesters of the program. GPA shows recent performance; CGPA shows overall academic trajectory. Both are important — a rising GPA trend with a lower CGPA shows improvement.
