Frequently Asked Questions

Are CalcDigi calculators free to use? +
Yes, 100% free. All calculators on CalcDigi are completely free with no sign-up, subscription, or hidden fees. You can use them as often as you like, including the AI-powered insights.
How do the AI insights work? +
After you run a calculation, our AI analyzes your specific numbers and provides a personalized explanation of what your results mean, whether they're in a healthy/optimal range, and practical tips. The AI assistant can also answer follow-up questions in real time.
How accurate are the health calculators? +
Our health calculators use the same evidence-based formulas used by medical professionals, including Mifflin-St Jeor for BMR/TDEE, NIH formulas for BMI, and the US Navy method for body fat. Results are estimates and should not replace professional medical advice.
Are the financial calculators updated for 2025? +
Yes. Our tax calculator uses the latest 2025 IRS federal income tax brackets. Mortgage and loan calculators use standard formulas and allow you to enter current interest rates for accurate results.
Do you store any data I enter into the calculators? +
No. All calculations happen directly in your browser. We do not collect, store, or share any values you enter into our calculators. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Can I use these calculators on my phone? +
Absolutely. CalcDigi is fully responsive and optimized for all devices including smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktops.
