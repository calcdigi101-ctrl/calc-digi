FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these health calculators free? +
Yes — all 35 health calculators on CalcDigi are completely free with no sign-up required. You can use them as many times as you like, including the AI-powered insights after every calculation.
How accurate are the health calculators? +
Our calculators use evidence-based formulas used by medical professionals — Mifflin-St Jeor for BMR/TDEE, NIH formulas for BMI, and the US Navy method for body fat. Results are estimates and should not replace professional medical advice.
What is the most popular health calculator? +
The BMI Calculator is our most used health tool, followed closely by the Calorie Calculator and TDEE Calculator. The Macro Calculator and Weight Loss Calculator are also extremely popular among fitness enthusiasts.
Do you store my health data? +
No. All calculations happen directly in your browser. We do not collect, store, or share any values you enter into our calculators. Your health data stays completely private.
