Our Mission

We believe everyone deserves access to powerful, accurate calculation tools — not just people who can afford premium apps or professional consultations. CalcDigi Now was built with one goal: to give every American access to the same quality of calculation tools previously only available in professional software.

From calculating a healthy BMI to planning mortgage payments to understanding compound interest, we've built every calculator with care, accuracy, and your privacy in mind. No sign-ups. No ads that track you. No selling your data. Just fast, accurate answers.

What We Offer

Health Calculators — BMI, BMR, TDEE, calorie, body fat, ideal weight, water intake, protein intake, heart rate zones, weight loss, lean body mass, and macros. All use evidence-based formulas from sources like the National Institutes of Health and peer-reviewed medical research.

Finance Calculators — Mortgage, auto loan, personal loan, compound interest, savings, retirement (with 4% rule), credit card payoff, US income tax (IRS 2025 brackets), salary converter, and inflation calculator. Designed specifically for the US financial system.

Math Calculators — Percentage, fraction, scientific, average (with mean/median/mode/std dev), ratio, and probability. Fast, accurate, and easy to use.

Medical & Financial Disclaimer

CalcDigi Now is an informational tool, not a substitute for professional advice. Health calculators provide statistical estimates — not medical diagnoses. Financial calculators provide projections — not financial advice. Always consult a licensed professional for decisions that matter. Read our full Disclaimer and Terms of Service.
