2. Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Information: When you visit our Site, our servers may automatically log standard data provided by your web browser, including your IP address, browser type and version, pages you visit, time and date of your visit, and time spent on each page.

Calculator Inputs: All calculations on CalcDigi Now are performed locally in your browser. We do not transmit, store, or collect any personal data (such as weight, age, height, or financial figures) that you enter into our calculators. Your data stays on your device.

Cookies: We use a small first-party cookie solely to remember your cookie consent preference. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies.

Analytics: We may use anonymized analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand aggregate usage patterns. This data is not linked to personally identifiable information.

When you use these features, a prompt describing your calculator result (but not your name or identifying information) is sent to Anthropic's servers to generate a response. We recommend reviewing Anthropic's Privacy Policy for information on how they handle API data.
